# Starving Vilgeismar

## 🌫️ The Miracle & The Veil

For four months, the castle-city of Vilgeismar has endured a full military siege. On the night the outer gate was breached and defenders faltered, the High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister gathered in the Cathedral of Twelve Faces and performed the **Hymn of Twelve Faces**. 

During the final verse, a pale, luminous veil formed over the city and extended beyond the walls.
* **Appearance:** A wide dome with a pale iridescence that shifts subtly from pearl to dull silver.
* **Function:** It prevents hostile approach and deflects projectiles and fire. It does not provide food, restore the dead, or repair damage.
* **Side Effects:** Some citizens report physical sensations when standing near the barrier, including dizziness, emotional intensity, or nosebleeds.

## 🙏 The Many Faced Lady
The patron deity of Vilgeismar is a monotheistic god understood through twelve widely accepted incarnations.

### Key Aspects
* **The Mother of Mercy:** Comfort, healing, and shelter. Symbol: Open hands.
* **The Veiled Judge:** Moral reckoning; sees all acts without passion. Symbol: Balanced scales behind a veil.
* **The Keeper of the Hearth:** Sustainer of home and community. Prayers to this face have grown strained during the famine. Symbol: Small flame in a stone bowl.
* **The Lady of Ash:** Aspect of loss and purification through destruction. Symbol: A blackened rose.
* **The Maiden of Chains:** Endurance in suffering and resilience. Symbol: Broken shackles held together by light.
* **The Hidden Face:** Represents what mortals cannot understand. It has no icon; altars leave a blank space.

## 📍 Points of Strain

### The Cathedral of Twelve Faces
The spiritual center of the city which now operates nearly without pause. Faith has intensified into rigidity, emphasizing endurance through suffering.
> [!warning] Concern
> Some congregants report unusually vivid dreams following extended hymnals.

### Beggar's Court & Butcher's Row
The primary ration distribution site. While the square is crowded and orderly, whispers circulate regarding disappearances among the city's most vulnerable residents.

### The Empty Granary
A structure guarded to prevent unrest. Public suspicion of hoarding persists; three merchants have been executed in the past month following mob accusations.

### The Well of First Water
The primary source of clear water. Residents report it tastes unusually sweet or metallic since the Miracle. Some claim drinking it temporarily reduces hunger.

## 👤 Key Figures

* **High Lady Seraphine Vaelor:** The symbolic center of Vilgeismar. She is worn by fasting but remains authoritative. Privately, she is troubled by reports of subtle changes within the city.
* **Captain Roderic Hale:** Commands the thinning ranks of defenders. He is pragmatic and trusts steel more than theology. He fears desperation within the walls as much as the enemy without.
* **Garron Thrice-Garrotted:** Operates from the shadows of Butcher's Row. He controls the illicit trade in hidden food supplies. Rumors suggest his "product" comes from the unclaimed dead or those who vanish.
* **Old Marta Kell:** A widow of Beggar's Court who commands quiet respect. She trusts lived experience over official proclamations and keeps track of those who disappear.

## 🕒 The Story So Far
For four months, the castle-city of Vilgeismar has endured a full military siege. Surrounding farms were burned, trade routes severed, and granaries emptied until hunger became the city's defining condition. Seven days ago, as the outer gate splintered and defenders faltered, High Lady Seraphine and the Sisters gathered in the Cathedral and performed the **Hymn of Twelve Faces**. 

During the final verse, a pale, iridescent veil formed over the walls. The enemy army reported terror and physical resistance, withdrawing beyond visible range by dawn. While the "Miracle" protects the city from fire and steel, the famine remains.

# 🔍 Log: Oddities & Supernatural Symptoms

This log tracks the "Physical Toll" and unexplained changes affecting the city since the Miracle appeared seven days ago.

### 🩸 Physical & Sensory Anomalies
* **The Weeping Wall Effect:** Citizens standing near the interior of the city walls report dizziness, intense emotional shifts, and nosebleeds.
* **Altered Taste:** The water from the Well of First Water now tastes unusually sweet or metallic.
* **The Sensation of Hunger:** Residents insist that drinking the well water or participating in communal hymnals temporarily reduces the physical sensation of hunger.
* **The Smell of the City:** The air carries a "sour tang of boiled leather" and a "metallic and faintly sweet" scent that clings to clothing and breath.

### 🧠 Psychological & Spiritual Shifts
* **Vivid Dreaming:** Members of the clergy and those attending extended hymnals report experiencing unusually vivid dreams.
* **Unnatural Endurance:** A minority of citizens and several Sisters of the Cloister have begun fasting for days beyond normal physical limits without collapsing.
* **The Unfamiliar Hymn:** Congregants have noted that the final verse of the *Hymn of Twelve Faces* feels unfamiliar, despite no written record of the lyrics being changed.
* **Unusual Calm:** Observers note an eerie, "unusual calm" among the weakest citizens in Beggar's Court—a slow reluctance of the underfed that borders on the instinctual.

### 🌌 The Veil's Behaviour
* **Visual Distortion:** The barrier appears as a shimmering heat distortion in early light, becoming a pale, iridescent dome as the sun rises.
* **Shifting Colours:** The surface shifts from pearl to dull silver or opaline when observed for too long; it does not flicker or waver.
* **Localized Density:** The veil appears at its thickest directly above the Gatehouse, the site of the final assault before the Miracle.

## 🕒 The Story So Far

*Starving Vilgeismar
For four months, the castle-city of Vilgeismar has endured a full military siege. The
surrounding farms were burned early in the campaign, trade routes were severed, and supply caravans never arrived. The granaries have been emptied, livestock slaughtered, and every viable reserve rationed to exhaustion. Hunger has become the defining condition of daily life.

On the night the outer gate was breached and the defending forces began to fail, the High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister gathered in the Cathedral of Twelve Faces and performed the Hymn of Twelve Faces in full choral prayer. During the final verse, a pale, luminous veil formed over the city and extended beyond the walls. Enemy forces attempting to advance reported terror, disorientation, and physical resistance. By dawn, the besieging army had withdrawn beyond visible range.
The barrier remains in place. It prevents hostile approach and deflects projectiles and fire. It does not provide food, restore the dead, or repair the damage already done.

The People of Vilgeismar refer to this even simply as the Miracle.
