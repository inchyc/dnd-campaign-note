# 🛡️ Item: The Mimic Ring


> [!summary] **Description** 
> At first glance, it appears to be a heavy silver band. Upon closer inspection, the "setting" is actually a tiny, hyper-realistic mouth with microscopic teeth. It feels unnervingly warm to the touch, almost as if it has a pulse.

### ✨ Properties & Effects

- **Mechanical (Suspected):** * **Copycat:** May allow the wearer to mimic the properties of another magical ring worn on the same hand.
    
    - **The Bite:** Could deal piercing damage to the wearer if attuned improperly or "starved."
        
- **Narrative:** It seems to "salivate" slightly when near high-potency magical items or the [[Valcara]]'s [[Item - Healing Honey (Greater)]].
    

### 🔍 Investigation & Lore

- **Resonance:** The ring hummed in the presence of the **Hymn of Birth**. This suggests it might be a "living" magical construct, similar to the Grain Monster.
    
- **The "Trap" Theory:** Was it left in the Vaults to "eat" the grain, or is it a tool for someone to siphon off the city's magical energy?
    
- **Origin:** This does not look like Cloister or Garrison craftsmanship. It feels "alien" to Vilgeismar's standard religious iconography.
    

### 🧪 Usage Log / Experiments

- [ ] **Feed Test:** Place the ring near a small scrap of food or a drop of blood to see if the "mouth" reacts.
    
- [ ] **Hymn Test:** Sing a low verse of the **Hymn of Death** to see if the ring cowers or becomes cold.
    
- [ ] **Identification:** Ask [[Rooprecht]] or a specialist in Beggar's Court if they've seen "flesh-metal" jewelry before.
