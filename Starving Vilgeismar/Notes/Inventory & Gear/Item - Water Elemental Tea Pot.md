# 🛡️ Item: Water Elemental Tea Pot

Markdown


> [!summary] **Description** 
> A heavy, ceramic teapot decorated with swirling blue patterns. It feels perpetually cool and you can hear the faint sound of a rushing stream inside, even when empty.

### ✨ Properties & Effects

- **Mechanical:** Can produce up to 1 gallon of fresh, clean water per day.
    
- **Narrative:** Unlike [[TTRPG/D&D/Starving Vilgeismar/Notes/Locations/The Well of First Water]], this water does **not** have a metallic or sweet taste. It is pure.
    

### 🔍 Investigation & Lore

- **Utility:** In a city where the main water source is "suspect" and causing hunger suppression, this is one of the party's most strategic assets.
    
- **Risks:** If the [[Garrison ]] or [[The Cloister ]] find out you have a source of pure water that bypasses the "Miracle" water, they may try to seize it.
