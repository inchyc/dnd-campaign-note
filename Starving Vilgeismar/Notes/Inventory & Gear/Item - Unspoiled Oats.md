# 🛡️ Item: Unspoiled Oats


> [!summary] **Description** 
> A small pouch of perfectly preserved, golden oats. They look out of place amidst the grey, fermented rot of the rest of the Granary.

### ✨ Properties & Effects

- **Narrative:** These oats serve as **Physical Evidence**. They prove that the spoilage in the city is not a natural result of the siege, but a targeted magical attack or "selection" process.
    

### 🔍 Investigation & Lore

- **The Question:** Why were _these_ specific oats left alone while the rest turned into a monster?
    
- **Next Step:** Present these to [[Sonnenblat]] or [[Guard Captain Roderic Hale]] to prove the "Grain Spoilage" is an intentional act of sabotage/magic.
