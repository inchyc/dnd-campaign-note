# 👤 Name: Garron Thrice-Garrotted

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio** 
> The ruthless leader of the criminal underworld in Butcher's Row. He controls the illicit trade of food and supplies during the famine.

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations

- **Physicality:** Dangerous and intimidating; recently bit a finger off a butcher during an interaction.
    
- **Supernatural Signs:** Suspected **Lycanthrope** (Werewolf) based on physical behavior and combat observations.
    
- **Territory:** Operates out of Butcher's Row, specifically associated with "The Abattoir" bar.
    

### 🤝 Relationship & Interactions

- **Current Status:** **Fragile Truce**.
    
- **The Agreement:** The party stays out of Butcher's Row; in exchange, Garron keeps his "less inhibited" men out of Beggar's Court. The party is not responsible for injuries to his men if they stray too far into the city.
    
- **Interaction History:** The party recently defeated his enforcers at Valcara’s house but successfully negotiated a way out of the conflict upon his arrival.
    

### 🤫 Secrets & Rumors

- **The "Product":** Rumors suggest his hidden food supplies may come from the unclaimed dead or those who vanish in Butcher's Row.
    
- **The Informant:** He is the source of the claim that **Captain Roderic Hale** is a traitor seeking martial law.
    
- **Connections:** His men were spotted harassing **Valcara**, potentially interested in her "harvesting" methods.
