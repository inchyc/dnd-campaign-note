# 👤 Name: Sonnenblat

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio** 
> An elderly half-elf tasked with the monumental burden of managing Vilgeismar’s dwindling grain supplies. He oversees the vaulted storage beneath the city.

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations

- **Physicality:** Described as looking "older than time," a testament to the years he has spent in the dark, cold vaults.
 
- **Personality:** Professional and cautious; he is a bureaucrat of survival who understands the weight of every grain.

- **Role:** Custodian of the three massive, steel-vaulted chambers that hold the city's lifeblood.


### 🤝 Relationship & Interactions

- **Interaction History:** He allowed the party access to the vaults to investigate reports of spoilage and strange noises.

- **Current Standing:** Currently under 'care' at [[The Cathedral of Twelve Faces]] after we found him unconscious next to his dead guards upon exiting the [[The Granary Vault]].
	- 

### 🤫 Secrets & Rumors

- **The Spoilage:** He was the one who initially noted that the grain was rotting in an "unnatural" way, despite being kept in sealed, climate-controlled vaults.

- **Merchant Executions:** He is aware that public panic over his dwindling supplies led to the execution of three merchants this month for suspected hoarding.
