# 👤 NPC: Lord Henrik Vaust

> [!abstract] **Quick Summary** 
> A minor noble residing in the Inner Ward of Vilgeismar. He is a prominent figure within the noble hierarchy who focuses on maintaining the appearance of stability and high-society culture despite the dire conditions of the siege.

### 🕵️ Observations & Intelligence

* **Physical Appearance**: Unlike the vast majority of the city’s population, Henrik Vaust appears noticeably "better nourished". While others are described as having sharp collarbones and a skeletal appearance, he maintains an image of health that contradicts the four-month famine.
* **Social Role**: He hosts small gatherings for other members of the elite. He frames these events as a necessity to preserve "civic morale" and the traditional dignity of the noble class.
* **Location**: He operates from the elevated manor houses of the [[The Noble Quarter]], specifically within the restricted Inner Ward.
* He is a Vampire.
* He was using us to get at [[High Lady Seraphine Vaelor]]. He has some ongoing vendetta against the leader of the Church of [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]] for some slight many years ago. He likes to kill the one in charge of the church ever so often.
* We did 'Defeat' him, though there was no body and he did seem to 'escape' in a way.
* We do have his sword. We recently discovered that it is one of [[Lore - The Great Fangs]], which were apparently made for and given to the Great Vampire Lords a long time ago. 

### ⚠️ Investigation Leads

* **The Ration Mystery**: His healthy appearance raises significant questions about the source of his food. Investigation is needed to determine if he has a secret stockpile, is involved in [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]]'s illicit trade, or has other unnatural means of sustenance. 
	* Turns out he had a completely different source of sustenance - Blood, from people....
* **Political Stance**: Like many of his peers, he likely views the [[Faction - The High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister]] with a mix of reliance and resentment, as the "Miracle" has shifted the city's power balance toward the Church.
	* Turns out he likes to kill the High Lady of the Church of [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]] every now and then because of some old slight (probably rejected, fucking nerd).
