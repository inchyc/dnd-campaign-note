# 👤 Name: Aurelion

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio** 
> An Elf and the husband of **The Butcher**. He is confirmed to be a **Lycanthrope**, much like [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]].

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations

- **Nature:** As a Lycanthrope, he possesses supernatural strength and senses that likely aid in the Underworld’s control over [[Butcher's Row]].
    
- **Status:** His marriage to the Butcher creates a powerful core alliance at the top of the criminal hierarchy.
    

### 🤝 Relationship & Interactions

- **Partnership:** Together with his husband, he forms the primary support structure for Garron's leadership.
    
- **Garrison Conflict:** Given his nature, he likely has a high degree of animosity toward [[Guard Captain Roderic Hale]] and the military forces.
    

### 🤫 Secrets & Rumors

- **The Infection:** It is unconfirmed if his lycanthropy is a result of his connection to Garron or if they share a common pack origin.
    
- **Disappearance Role:** Given his Elven lifespan and Lycanthropic abilities, he may be the one "selecting" or tracking the missing citizens from [[Beggar's Court]].
- Rapid healing from any damage from non silvered weapons.
