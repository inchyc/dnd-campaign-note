# 👤 Name: The Butcher

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio** 
> A massive Orc who serves as [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]]’s second-in-command. He manages the daily operations of [[The Abattoir]] and oversees the "processing" within the district.

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations

- **Role:** Second-in-command to Garron; effectively the "enforcer" of the Underworld's interests.

- **Visuals:** Usually found in [[The Abattoir]] beneath the gigantic Orcish tribal tapestry.

- **Demeanor:** Professional and efficiet; he maintains the "unusually clean" standards of the bar despite the surrounding squalor.
 

### 🤝 Relationship & Interactions

- **Loyalty:** Deeply loyal to Garron and his husband, Aurelion.
    
- **The Manager:** He is the primary point of contact for anyone seeking an audience with the "notable figures" of the Underworld.
    

### 🤫 Secrets & Rumors

- **The Tapestry:** Likely belongs to his original tribe; its presence in the bar suggests a deep personal history or a claim of territory.
    
- **The "Parties":** He is likely the one coordinating the logistics for the residential houses where guests vanish.
