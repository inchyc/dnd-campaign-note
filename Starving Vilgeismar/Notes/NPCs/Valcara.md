# 👤 Valcara

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio** An elven beekeeper with an unusual temperament who maintains the city gardens. She is described as being "a little off" (potentially neurodivergent).

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations

- **Physicality:** An elf who manages a collection of bees producing various magical honeys.
 
- **Supernatural Signs:** Her father was likely a Warlock.

- **Environment:** She keeps the gardens "surprisingly clean," though they carry a heavy, musky scent.

- Seems to be quite an accomplished magic user. Is well enough versed in 'Tribe' 'Hedge Magic' (Goblin, Orc, Centaur). 

- Seems to have an extreme dislike of goblins and enjoyed blowing it up. Though she also seemed extremely fascinated about it, whether about the capability of the goblin or the goblin itself we have yet to determine.

- Has an interesting wand seeming made of 2 twigs that look to have been forcibly twisted together.

- Quite knowledgeable on 'Tribe' 'Hedge Magic'.


### 🤝 Relationship & Interactions

- **First Met:** In the gardens during the disappearance investigation.

- **Interaction History:** The party fought off Garron’s enforcers at her home.

- **Current Agreements:**
	- She provided the party with various honeys and explained her "harvesting" methods.
	- Is amendable to helping us with some of our magic related investigations. Helped significantly in our [[Grain Spoilage]] investigation.



### 🤫 Secrets & Rumors

- **The Bee Pact:** Claims she has an agreement with the High Lady to use the bodies of those who starved to death to feed her bees.
 
- **Harvesting:** The party discovered poorly buried bodies in her garden being "harvested" by her bees.

- **Father**: Was likely a warlock

- Clearly an accomplished magic user herself. (Elven Magic)
