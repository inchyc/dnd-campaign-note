# 👤 Demire AKA The Traveller

> [!abstract] **Quick Bio**
> "Not all those who wander are lost"
> Those that are lost tend to find Demire, or is it he who finds those who are lost?

### 📝 Key Traits & Observations
- Is the first of [[Lore - The Little Gods|The Little Gods]].
- Always talks about being lost.
- Is also known as the Traveller by his followers.
- Can turn into a Black Cat, and can change his size in this form.
- Has some strong magic. Some of which allows him to use The Old Gods as conduits.
- Very eccentric.
- Owns a [[Lore - Demire's Boat|Boat]]. The boat is Captained by [[NPC - Glutenboss|Glutenboss]] a talking Monkey.
- [[Lore - Demire's Boat|Demire's Boat]] has a constant steam of [[Lore - Lost|Lost]] things flowing onto it, of which [[NPC - Glutenboss|Glutenboss]] sorts.
