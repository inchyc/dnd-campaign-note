# 🫂 Party Member: Vex

> [!abstract] **Quick Summary** 
> A traditional, unblinking Paladin of the Many-Faced Lady. Vex serves as the "Silent Witness," an observer in the Cathedral and a guardian for the High Lady. To the party, Vex is the group's moral anchor and physical shield.

### ⚔️ Combat & Utility

- **Signature Move:** Divine Smite / Oath of Vengeance. Vex provides sudden and extreme violence if the High Lady or the innocent are put in direct danger.
    
- **Unique Skills:** Expert in religious observation and the theological extremes of the church. They possess an unnerving, unblinking gaze that is used to monitor crowds at the Well of First Water.
    
- **Resource Tracking:** Heavy plate armor featuring a Single Open Eye on the chest; wears a mask where only the eyes are visible.
    

### 🤝 Narrative & Connections

- **Relationship with Me:** Viewed as a necessary, if unnerving, pillar of the Church. My character recognizes Vex as the High Lady’s "private solution" to public anxieties.
    
- **Motivations:** To fulfill the will of the Many-Faced Lady and protect the interests of the Cloister at any cost.
    
- **Key Interactions:** Presence at the Cathedral confessionals and the gate during the Miracle. Vex was a participant in the initial Hymn that created the barrier.
    

### 🕵️ Secrets & Theories

- **Suspicions:** While Vex appears to be a loyal Human Paladin, their reflexes and shape-changing abilities are becoming strained by the prolonged hunger, which may eventually reveal their true nature to the party.
    
- **Current Theory:** Vex believes the barrier is the unquestionable will of the Many-Faced Lady, though they maintain a "trust, yet verify" attitude toward everyone else in the city.
