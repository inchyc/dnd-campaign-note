# Character: Nonna Rossini (The Great Wyrm)
**Concept:** An ancient dragon with dementia who thinks she's just a grumpy Italian grandmother visiting her daughter.
**Class:** Barbarian (Path of the Ancestral Guardian) 10
**Race:** Variant Human

---

## 1. Attributes (Standard Array)
| Stat    | Score  | Mod | Roleplay Context                           |
| :------ | :----- | :-- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **STR** | **16** | +3  | The "Old Lady Grip" (Dragon strength).     |
| **DEX** | **14** | +2  | Deceptively fast for her age.              |
| **CON** | **14** | +2  | Tough as old boots; survives anything.     |
| **INT** | **8**  | -1  | The Dementia. Often forgets the century.   |
| **WIS** | **12** | +1  | Ancient intuition, though easily confused. |

---

## 2. The Core Mechanics
### The "Ear Grab" (Grappling God)
* **Skill Expert (Athletics):** At Level 4, take the *Skill Expert* feat.
* **The Result:** You have **Expertise in Athletics**. Even as a "small lady," your grapple check is +11 (with advantage while raging). You don't just grab ears; you anchor them to the earth with dragon-tier force.

### The "Naughty Step" (Path of the Ancestral Guardian)
* **Ancestral Protectors (Lvl 3):** The first enemy you hit has disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you.
    * *Flavor:* You aren't summoning ghosts; your dragon aura is subconsciously warping space to protect the "grandkids" (the party).
* **Spirit Shield (Lvl 6):** Use your reaction to reduce damage to an ally by 2d6 (3d6 at lvl 10).
    * *Flavor:* You shout "Watch your step, dearie!" and the air literally hardens into invisible scales to block the blow.

---

## 3. Feats & Progression
* **Level 1 (Human Feat): Magic Initiate (Sorcerer).**
    * *Prestidigitation:* Cleaning aprons and seasoning food with "magic."
    * *Blade Ward:* You think you're "just lucky," but your skin is as hard as dragon hide.
    * *Shield (1/day):* A reflex reality-warp that stops a "naughty" weapon in mid-air.
* **Level 4 Feat: Skill Expert.** (+1 Strength, Athletics Expertise).
* **Level 8 Feat: Sentinel.** If they try to run from your lecture, their speed becomes 0. "I wasn't finished with you, young man!"

---

## 4. Playstyle: The Two Perspectives

| Action | What Nonna Sees | What the Party Sees |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rage** | Getting a bit "red in the face" because people are being rude. | Her shadow grows wings, her eyes glow amber, and the ground cracks. |
| **Grapple** | Reaching up to pull a tall bandit down by his ear for a scolding. | Space warps around her hand as she slams a 300lb man into the dirt. |
| **Attack** | A firm "thwack" with a heavy wooden rolling pin (Warhammer). | A brutal, bone-crushing strike that carries the weight of a dragon's tail. |
| **Reckless** | Ignoring her "aching back" to teach a lesson. | She ignores a sword to the gut as if it were a mosquito bite. |

---

## 5. Roleplay Quirks
* **The Hoard:** She pickpockets "pretty rocks" (gems) and shiny spoons from dungeons to give to her daughter. She doesn't realize she's hoarding.
* **Mistaken Identity:** She calls the party’s Fighter "Ignis" (her firstborn Red Dragon) and scolds him for "playing with fire" when he uses a torch.
* **The "Hunch":** She is perpetually bent over, but when she grabs someone, she doesn't reach up—the world seems to *bend down* to meet her hand.
