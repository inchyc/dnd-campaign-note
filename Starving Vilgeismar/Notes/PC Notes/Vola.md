# 🫂 Party Member: Vola

> [!abstract] **Quick Summary** A silent and observant Half-Orc Barbarian who serves as the party's stoic muscle and Erevan’s dedicated protector.

### ⚔️ Combat & Utility

- **Signature Move:** Primal transformations (Path of the Beast) and heavy melee engagement.

- **Unique Skills:** Expert at blending into the background despite her stature; keen observational skills.

- **Resource Tracking:** Wears ragged travel gear and a hooded cloak to conceal her identity and tusks.


### 🤝 Narrative & Connections

- **Relationship with Me:** Vola remains largely in the background, though her silence often serves as a grounded counterpoint to group antics.

- **Motivations:** Bound by a debt to Erevan after being caught stealing from his shop; she fights to keep her outsider status a secret.

- **Key Interactions:** Primarily focused on her role as a bodyguard; rarely engages with Erevan's humor.


### 🕵️ Secrets & Theories

- **Suspicions:** She is an outsider with a secret she is desperate to protect, leading to her constant use of a hood to hide her face.

- **Current Theory:** Likely views **The Miracle** or the **Bee Pact** through a lens of pragmatic survival rather than religious tradition.
