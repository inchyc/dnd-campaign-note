# 👤 Hidden Face: Vesper

> [!abstract] **Quick Summary** 
> The "unseen scalpel" for the High Lady. Vesper is the persona adopted at night to handle "immediate problems" that the public-facing Church cannot touch[cite: 3]. While Vex observes, Vesper hunts[cite: 3].

### ⚔️ Combat & Utility

- **Signature Move:** Final Judgment. Vesper hunts those directed by the High Lady or those whose sins are deemed too harmful to the Church.
    
- **Unique Skills:** Expert in infiltration and elimination[cite: 3]. Uses an armor set reminiscent of the magical barrier covering Vilgeismar to blend into the unnatural surroundings.
    
- **Resource Tracking:** Colorless or shifting iris colors used to instill fear; gear is designed for maximum mobility and stealth, discarding all traditional church vestments.
    

### 🤝 Narrative & Connections

- **Relationship with Me:** The "Hidden Face" represents the darker, necessary reality of the siege that my character likely suspects exists but rarely sees in the light.
    
- **Motivations:** Serves as the private solution to Seraphine’s public anxieties. Vesper believes they are the only ones capable of the "unpleasant" necessities required for the Church's survival.
    
- **Key Interactions:** Operates in the Inner Cloister and the shadows of the city. Vesper remains a phantom to most, as those who see them clearly rarely survive to speak of it.
    

### 🕵️ Secrets & Theories

- **The True Nature:** Vesper is the persona that most closely reflects the Changeling nature, though the party is entirely unaware that this "assassin" and their Paladin companion are the same individual.
    
- **Current Theory:** Vesper views the Miracle as a tactical advantage but fears the baser survival instincts of the citizenry will eventually fracture the city's morale.
