## Changeling Paladin

Name: Vex/Vesper
Race: Changeling
Class: Paladin
Oath: Oath of Vengence

# Overview:

My Function is as the unseen scalpel for the High Lady. I fill a function few can due to the advantages of my rarely seen race, balancing the public facing requirements of faith with the necessities of survival for the Church and those deemed worthy.

Using my nature as a changeling I embody possibly two of the most theological distinct extremes of the Many Faced Lady:

## **Public Face: The Silent Witness**

- The persona adopted by day and physically looking very much like what would be expected of a traditional paladin. 
- My main duty is generally an unblinking observer in the Cathedral of Twelve Faces, frequently found near the confessionals, in meetings of a more sensitive nature, yet is it not abnormal for me to be near the Well of First Water watching the crowds an unblinking, unnerving gaze.
- My Armour has a Single Open Eye on the chest and I wear a Mask with only my eyes visible with the Single Open Eye also displayed in the position traditionally reserved for Third Eye. It is rare that I will speak to any but the High Lady unless needed (such as direct questioning/investigating needed), or in the rarest of cases my ability for sudden and extreme violence isn't heeded or ignored when someone puts the High Lady in direct danger.

## **Hidden Face: The Name of the Unnamed**

- The persona adopted mostly at night or when more immediate "problems" arise. My normal church vestments are discarded as I become an aspect of the Hidden Face, that those yet to reach enlightenment (full acceptance of the the church) cannot understand and those that have only invoke in the most solemn and sacred of rituals.
- With the actions of this persona since the siege began (as the area of operation has naturally been restricted) those that argue that invoking the danger only brings danger have seeming had their suspicions confirmed and these thoughts are slowly starting to spread.
- I Hunt those that I have been directed to by the High Lady (though I won't do so without knowing the reason), or those whose secrets, confessions or sin I have deemed too harmful to the Church.
- My Armour/Outfit would be more inline with that expected of assassin, though those that have been close enough to see it properly would tell you that it's not black its more reminiscent of the barrier currently covering Vigesimal, the only problem is that those that have seen it that close are unfortunately no longer capable of comminating that. Only my eyes are visible and I will often have two different iris colours or have them colourless, depending on the level of fear deemed appropriate before final judgement is carried out.

## Key Figures

**High Lady Seraphine Vaelor**

I am the Churches solution to problems that cannot truly be solved by the public facing side. This has made me Seraphine's private solution to her public anxieties. While she obviously presents the Miracle as a Mercy, she fears the nature and basic survival instincts of the many citizens may lead to issues that will fracture the morale of the already teetering city.

I report to her in the restricted Inner Cloister, I enter in a form that she alone recognizes and is the only time she sees my face, not that it is my true face, nor even one of the ones I would generally use in public even before the siege. Being the Inner Cloister it is a female face and figure, one that could obviously fit with my daytime persona as the Silent Witness, it is a body obviously capable of wearing the armour that I wear. This is to stop questions when it comes to being a Silent Witness for the High Lady when required.

Other than that though, I am a strangely unremarkable presence at this time. Neither attractive or unattractive, I don't stand out and almost all forget me the moment I leave their sight.

The High Lady knows what I do, and utilizes me needed. Yet the methods are mine and I have carried out all she has asked so far as I found her judgment to be sound. 

At the end of the day though, my own judgement is what ultimately will decide what I do, as many others in the past have tried to utilize me for their own means, not the churches, the have found out that the Hidden Face serves none but the Many Faced Lady.

I have made all efforts to ensure she doesn't know the true nature of my race, just assuming I am Human, even if I have a few quirks.

## Background

Criminal - Spy

Trying to fit in during my youth. I naturally used and perfected my skills of change and learned that just looking like someone or even looking similar was often not enough to fit it. Initially it led me to what most would consider the darker side of Enlighted beings, the criminal underworld. 

Many who think that though do not truly have experience with them though, and would be even more surprised to realise how many of 'those' types they deal with on a day to day basis, completely unaware. Nevertheless a lot of them have moral codes of their own that many would find offensive, yet they show how much Good and Evil truly sit on a spectrum.

Because of this. in the early years working within this world, I saw what truly being able to not only fit in, but be accepted and even welcomed into all different aspects of society. Learning these skills, habits and behaviours, coupled with my ability to change my appearance at will. I quickly became someone sort after to handle many situations, especially delicate ones.

One such job led to something, that thinking back, would if I had ended up following up, truly ended up being of no consequence, some people think so small. This job led me to the Many Faced Lady. 

My only regret with this job was the initial entry to it. It is unfortunate that a Sister had to give her life. though the good that has come from that, most Sisters would gladly give their life for. Who knows maybe the Many-Faced Lady orchestrated the whole thing?

Once I had integrated into the Church, strangely I finally felt like I belonged. The Many Faced Lady, the many faces I can take, it just felt it was meant to be.

So I intended to stay and worked to becoming a Silent Witness given my skillset. It was a quick and easy rise. The thing growing up with criminals though is that there is almost a smell to it, a smell I quickly found unsavoury things happening within the Church. 

The head of the local chapter was informed, anonymously, as you never know how high corruption can go. I watched and waited for action to be taken. Eventually, I chose to act, the unsavoury ventured into truly Evil territory. To distance myself from the Church while I acted I took a male form that you would expect on the battlefield fearlessly leading the charge, confident in their physical ability.

Moving back to earlier roots, I could have been mistaking as a thief, if I was seen. Confronting these people, there was one other thing I had done, likely considered blasphemy for most, I wore a mask that represents the Hidden Face. The last thing these truly evil beings saw was that Mask, with the eyes of my true form looking from behind it. From the looks I saw as the final light left their eyes, the received my and the Many Faced Lady's message.

Leaving the scene, about to remove the mask and go back to my daily normal to monitor if more action needed to be taken, I felt a presence. Turning I must admit I was surprised to be facing the Head of the local church, speaking, she said she had been watching those that I had just left based on information that I had anomalously given. She was hoping to see if the ones that had provided had the potential she was looking for, and was surprised to see me there, especially given the mask I was wearing.

Many hours later, it turned out I now had another roll in the Church, it seems that I had given the Church an even better way to handle 'issues' within the Church.

While there is much in between then and now, much of it you can probably put together yourself if you're high enough in the church and can read the history between the lines. In between those lines is where I belong.

The only difference now is that the the High Lady of whichever branch/city I am currently at knows of both my roles as the Silent Face and the Hidden Face. Depending on them, there is generally some arrangement where I meet them in a way that my association with the Church isn't obvious. 

Currently with High Lady Seraphine Vaelor, I meet with here in the form of a women, completely plain in everyway. These meetings are all prearranged and generally are the way to pass the right direction onto the The Hidden Face, this is because as far as they are aware, these two roles have some strange link, hence why there is always the two roles. They couldn't be the same person, as one is female and one is male afterall.

No one in the Church, the whole Church or this city has ever seen my true form or knows my true nature.

Many think the guide or control me, I am the only one truly making the decisions that I do though.


## Questions:

- **What role did you occupy before the siege?**

The same as I have always done in the church. Given the current situation, unsurprisingly I have a lot more to do since the siege began,

- **How has prolonged hunger affected you physically and spiritually?**

Being somewhat familiar with hunger, even prolonged hunger mentally I have been to mostly ignore the constant feel of hunger and the spirally thoughts and reversion to baser instincts that I have been witnessing in others. That being said, this prolonged hunger is starting weigh on me physically, I have noticed reflexes not being up to my usual standard and lately I have also noticed it is starting to require more willpower to change my shape physically, where normally it would done with barely a thought.

- **Did you participate in the Hymn during the Miracle?**

I did during the initial event as I was at the gate with the defenders, I have done so since as all Sisters of the church are required to be. The schedule is slightly less restrictive on me due to the other tasks that need to be done.

- **Do you believe the barrier is unquestionably divine?**

I believe it is the will of the Many Faced Lady.

- **What would you do to preserve the city?**

Any that know my history, would know I would anything to fulfil the will of the Many Faced Lady, which generally means protecting the interests of the Church. If the Church deems the City necessary to be preserved, I will do what I must, unless I come to believe the will of the Many Faced Lady is different.

- **What doubts, if any, do you keep private?**

I trust myself and the Many Faced Lady. I doubt everyone to an extent. Trust, yet verify is generally my modus operandi. Where I have worked with someone before, more inherent trust is given, nothing and no one is above scrutiny.
