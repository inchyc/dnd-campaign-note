# 📍 Name: The Cathedral of Twelve Faces

Markdown

> [!abstract] **Atmosphere** Thrumming with the constant sound of near-ceaseless hymnals; faith has intensified into a rigid, desperate endurance.

### 🔍 Points of Interest

- **The Altars:** Dedicated to the Twelve Faces of the Lady, including a blank space for the **Hidden Face**.
    
- **The Cloister:** The residence and headquarters of the Sisters and [[High Lady Seraphine Vaelor]].
    

### 👥 Local Inhabitants

- **Key NPC:** [[High Lady Seraphine Vaelor]].
    
- **The Sisters of the Cloister:** Religious leaders who performed the Hymn of Twelve Faces to trigger the Miracle.
