# 📍 The Well of First Water

> [!abstract] **Atmosphere**
> > The air nearby is "metallic and faintly sweet," carrying a "sour tang of boiled leather" that clings to your breath.

### 🔍 Points of Interest

- **The Water Source:** The primary source of clear water for the city residents.

- **Supernatural Signs:** Since the Miracle appeared, the water has taken on an unusually sweet or metallic taste.

- **Physical Effects:** Citizens report that drinking the water—or participating in nearby communal hymnals—temporarily reduces the physical sensation of hunger. The Church has been silent about this


### 👥 Local Inhabitants

- **The Weakened:** Residents of [[Beggar's Court]] frequent this well.

- **The Sisters:** Members of the Cloister often gather here for prayer and hymnals.

### 🕸️ Leads & Oddities

- [ ] **Psychosomatic or Poison:** Determine if the hunger suppression is a spiritual "Miracle," a psychological belief, or a literal poison.

- [ ] **The Scent Profile:** Investigate why the metallic scent of the water matches the scent lingering in [[Butcher's Row]].

- [ ] **Unnatural Endurance:** Check if those who drink exclusively from this well are the same people displaying "unnatural endurance" beyond physical limits.

- [ ] **[[Sonnenblat]]**: Made stops here every morning as part of his ritualistic behaviour. It is unknown to us what he was doing, though given the Granary Vault and his cane, we should investigate anyway.
