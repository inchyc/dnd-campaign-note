# 📍 The Hearth of Ash

The Hearth of Ash is situated in the middle tier of Vilgeismar, marking the meeting point between the **Artisan Quarter** and the narrow, descending streets of **Beggar’s Court**. Once a bustling guildhall and communal bakehouse, its massive brick ovens now stand cold more often than not.

### 🔍 Features

* **The Great Hearth**: A large brick structure that formerly burned from dawn until dusk to feed the district.
* **Communal Space**: It remains one of the few places in the city where residents gather without a formal religious or military summons.

### 🏛️ Social Atmosphere

* **The Protection Scheme**: A subtle criminal element has taken root within the hall. Unfamiliar faces often intervene to settle arguments quickly, and certain tables remain "reserved" without official explanation.
* **Shift in Authority**: Ration distributors in this location have begun deferring to a specific group of men when disputes arise.

### ⚠️ Current Concern

* **Criminal Consolidation**: The location has become a hub for the **[[Faction - The Quiet Doubters]]** and figures like **[[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]]**, who profit as the hunger in the city deepens.
* **Illicit Trade**: It is a primary site for the trade of "hidden" food supplies that bypass the official Cathedral ration lines.
