# 📍 The Noble Quarter

The Noble Quarter is an elevated district of manor houses belonging to the principal families of Vilgeismar. Situated in the upper tiers of the city, it was designed to project power, history, and architectural elegance, though much of its grandeur has been muted by the ongoing siege.

### 🔍 Features

* **Manor Estates**: Large stone estates that remain physically intact but are largely hollowed out due to a lack of servants, fuel, and supplies.
* **Elevated Position**: The district's height provides a clear view of the city below and the iridescent veil shimmering above the walls.
* **Inner Ward Access**: The district is closely tied to the Inner Ward, where the most influential families, such as those of Lord Henrik Vaust, reside.

### 🏛️ Social Atmosphere

* **Projected Stability**: The nobility continues to host private councils and small gatherings in an effort to maintain a veneer of cultured composure and "civic morale".
* **Silence and Stagnation**: Without the bustle of staff and the warmth of well-fed hearths, the district feels eerily still compared to its pre-siege state.
* **Symbolic Privilege**: Even as their material wealth erodes, the noble houses remain the gatekeepers of traditional social hierarchy.

### ⚠️ Current Concern

* **Growing Resentment**: Tensions are rising as the hungry populations in the lower tiers look upward with anger at the perceived maintenance of noble privilege.
* **Political Fragility**: The noble families are deeply divided on whether the Miracle has validated their right to rule or if the High Lady’s religious authority has permanently eclipsed them.
* **The "Nourished" Mystery**: Observations that some nobles, such as Henrik Vaust, appear better nourished than the rest of the starving population fuel rumors of hoarding.
