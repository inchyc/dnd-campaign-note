# 📍 Name: The Vaults (The Deep Granary)


> [!abstract] **Atmosphere** Oppressively dry air that carries a sour, fermented tang of rot. The silence is broken only by a low, rhythmic hum—like a vibration in the stone itself.

### 🔍 Points of Interest

- **The Steel Vaults:** Three massive, reinforced chambers. The aesthetic is heavy industrial/Victorian (heavy bolts, thick iron doors). Seems out of place considering the city itself.
    
- **The Spoilage Zone:** A specific area where a pile of grain was actively transforming into the "Grain Monster."
    
- **The Magic Field:** A lingering pocket of **Conjuration magic** that doesn't seem to have a clear source within the room.
    

### 👥 Local Inhabitants

- **Current Occupants:** None (Stationary guards remain at the entrance on the floor above).
    
- **Key NPC:** [[Sonnenblat]] (He rarely enters the deep sub-levels alone now).
    

### 🕸️ Leads & Oddities (Why it's Suspect)

- [ ] **The "Hymn" Connection:** The Grain Monster emitted the **Hymn of Birth**. If the High Lady's Miracle uses these Hymns, does that mean the "Miracle" is what's rotting the food?
    
- [ ] **The Conjuration Source:** Conjuration usually implies _bringing something from elsewhere_. Is the grain being stolen and replaced with rot, or is the rot being summoned?
    
- [ ] **The Resonance:** The metallic "sweetness" found at [[TTRPG/D&D/Starving Vilgeismar/Notes/Locations/The Well of First Water]] is also faintly present here.
    
- [ ] **Valcara's Analysis:** Need to bring [[Valcara]] down here. If she uses dead bodies to grow her garden, she might recognize if this "rot" is biological or purely magical.
    

### 🚩 Investigation Log

- **Combat Record:** Encountered a creature made of rotting grain.
    
- **Success:** Discovered that the **Hymn of Death** (The Lady of Ash's verse) causes the entity to disintegrate.
