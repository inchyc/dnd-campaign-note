# 📍 The Inner Cloister

The Inner Cloister is a restricted grounds attached to the Cathedral of Twelve Faces. It serves as the private residence and meditative sanctuary for the High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister. Entry is strictly limited to ordained clergy and invited officials.

### 🔍 Features

* **Sacred Seclusion**: Since the appearance of the Miracle, the grounds have become a site of intense isolation.
* **Liturgy of Silence**: The Sisters here devote extended hours to prayer and absolute silence, far removed from the public chaos of the city.

### 🏛️ Social Atmosphere

* **Withdrawal of Leadership**: High Lady Seraphine Vaelor resides almost entirely within these walls, rarely making public appearances since the barrier formed.
* **Devotional Discipline**: The atmosphere is defined by a rigorous, almost punishing level of spiritual commitment.

### ⚠️ Current Concern
* **Unnatural Vitality**: Several Sisters within the Cloister appear physically skeletal and weakened by starvation, yet they continue their duties without physical collapse.
* **Ceasing of Sustenance**: Rumors suggest that some within the inner circle have stopped eating altogether, an act described by the Church as "devotional discipline" rather than a symptom of the famine.
