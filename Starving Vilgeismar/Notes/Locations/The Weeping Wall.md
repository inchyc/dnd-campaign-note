# 📍 The Weeping Wall

The area along the interior side of the city walls where the iridescent barrier is most visible. It stands as a physical meeting point between the stone of Vilgeismar and the shimmering dome of the Miracle.

### 🔍 Features

* **Visibility**: The veil is most prominent here, particularly at dusk, appearing as a thick, iridescent shimmer against the sky.
* **The Atmosphere**: The air near the wall feels heavy and distorted, similar to the shimmer of heat above summer stone.

### 🏛️ Social Presence

* **Pilgrims**: Some citizens gather here to pray or observe the veil, viewing it as a shield of divine protection.
* **Avoidance**: Others avoid the area entirely, unsettled by the physical and emotional shifts felt in its proximity.

### ⚠️ Current Concern

* **Physical Toll**: Individuals standing close to the wall report physical sensations, including dizziness and nosebleeds.
