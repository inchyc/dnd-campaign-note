# 📍 The Artisan Quarter

The Artisan Quarter serves as the industrial and creative hub of Vilgeismar. Located in the city's middle tier, it historically housed the workshops, smithies, and guildhalls that fueled the city's economy before the siege began.

### 🔍 Features

* **Workshop Rows**: Narrow streets lined with the shuttered storefronts of weavers, potters, and carpenters.
* **Industrial Hearth**: The district is home to large-scale communal facilities, most notably the [[The Hearth of Ash]], which once served as the primary bakehouse for the area.
* **The Descent**: The terrain here begins to slope downward, with steep, narrow streets leading toward the lower elevations of Beggar’s Court.

### 🏛️ Social Atmosphere

* **Stalled Livelihoods**: Since the Miracle, most professional work has halted due to a total lack of raw materials and the prioritization of rations over trade.
* **The Quiet Shift**: Once characterized by the sound of hammers and looms, the district is now defined by a strained silence, broken only by the echo of hymns from the Cathedral.
* **Shadow Economy**: In the absence of official trade, the quarter has become a prime location for illicit dealings and the gathering of the [[Faction - The Quiet Doubters]].

### ⚠️ Current Concern

* **Poverty and Displacement**: Many artisans and tradespeople now rely entirely on communal charity and ration lines.
* **Criminal Rooting**: Protection schemes and underground markets have taken advantage of the cold hearths and empty guildhalls to establish new, unsanctioned centers of authority.
