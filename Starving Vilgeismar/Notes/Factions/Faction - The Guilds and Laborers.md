# 👥 Faction: The Guilds and Laborers

### 🕯️ Summary

The Guilds and Laborers represent the artisans, tradespeople, and common workers who once formed the backbone of Vilgeismar's economy. Since the onset of the siege and the appearance of the Miracle, their livelihoods have completely halted, leaving them displaced and reliant on communal charity.

---

### 🔍 Current Status

* **Economic Stagnation**: With trade routes severed and raw materials exhausted, traditional guild work has been replaced by a struggle for basic survival.
* **Ration Dependency**: This group is now primarily defined by their presence in ration lines and their reliance on the distributions managed by the Cathedral and Noble Houses.
* **Points of Gathering**: Many members frequent the [[The Hearth of Ash]] and [[Beggar's Court]] while waiting for news or food.

---

### ⚠️ Internal Tensions

* **Erosion of Identity**: The loss of their trade and the freezing of the communal hearths has led to a significant loss of social standing and purpose.
* **Vulnerability**: As the group most affected by the cessation of trade, they are particularly susceptible to the protection schemes and illicit trade managed by the [[Faction - The Quiet Doubters]].
* **Growing Resentment**: There is a quiet, simmering tension regarding past rationing decisions and the perceived hoarding of resources by the merchant class.
