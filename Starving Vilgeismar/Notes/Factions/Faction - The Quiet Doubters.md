# 👥 Faction: The Quiet Doubters

### 🕯️ Summary

The Quiet Doubters are a loose collection of citizens, primarily from the criminal underworld, who privately question the divine nature and long-term consequences of the Miracle. They operate in the shadows to avoid the religious rigidity of the Cathedral, where public doubt is often suppressed to maintain morale.

---

### 🔍 Beliefs & Motivation

* **Skepticism of the Veil**: Unlike the majority of the city, they do not believe the barrier is unquestionably divine or without a hidden cost.
* **Observation of Change**: They are among the first to note the "different taste" in the air and the unusual behavior of the water from the [[TTRPG/D&D/Starving Vilgeismar/Notes/Locations/The Well of First Water|The Well of First Water]].
* **Resource Preservation**: Many are tied to the illicit trade of food and supplies managed by figures like [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]].

---

### 📍 Areas of Influence

* **[[The Hearth of Ash]]**: A guildhall where a protection scheme has taken root and where Doubters can gather without formal summons.
* **Butcher's Row**: The center of the illicit meat trade and a frequent site for those who have lost faith in official rationing.
* **Beggar's Court**: Where commoners like [[Old Marta Kell]] keep track of those who "vanish" under the cover of the Miracle.

---

### ⚠️ Known Concerns

* **The Disappearances**: They are deeply concerned by the quiet vanishing of vulnerable residents from districts like Butcher's Row.
* **The "Unfamiliar" Verse**: Members have noted that the air feels heavier and the city's hymns have shifted in a way that feels unnatural.
* **Condition of the Miracle**: They fear the enemy has not retreated but has instead repositioned while the city is being changed from within.

---

### 🤝 Key Contacts

* **[[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]]**: Provides the illicit resources that allow this group to remain independent of Cathedral charity.
* **[[Old Marta Kell]]**: Though not an "official" member, her plain-spoken skepticism and tracking of the missing align with their goals.
