# 👥 Faction: The Noble Houses

### 🕯️ Summary

The landholding families of Vilgeismar who traditionally governed civic affairs and maintained the city's hierarchy. Despite the total erosion of their material wealth during the four-month siege, they struggle to preserve social order and the prestige of their lineages.

---

### 🔍 Current Status

* **Location**: They are primarily concentrated in the **Noble Quarter**, an elevated district of manor houses that remain largely intact but silent due to a lack of servants and supplies.
* **Civic Role**: These families continue to hold private councils to project an image of stability and cultured composure.
* **Charity & Loyalty**: Some houses distribute their remaining private stores as charity rations to maintain the loyalty of the lower classes.

---

### ⚠️ Internal Tensions

* **Divided Authority**: The nobility is increasingly split on whether the Miracle has strengthened their rule or completely undermined it in favor of the Cathedral's religious authority.
* **Public Resentment**: Growing anger is directed toward the Noble Quarter as hunger worsens, fueled by the perceived maintenance of privilege—even if only symbolic.
* **Erosion of Hierarchy**: As the hunger deepens, their traditional political authority is being challenged by the immediate survival needs of the population.

---

### 🤝 Key Figures

* **Lord Henrik Vaust**: A minor noble of the Inner Ward who hosts gatherings to preserve "civic morale" and appears noticeably better nourished than the average citizen.
* **High Lady Seraphine Vaelor**: Though religious, she remains the ultimate authority they must coordinate with.
