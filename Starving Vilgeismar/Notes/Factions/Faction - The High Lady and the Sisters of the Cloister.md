# 👥 Faction: The High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister

### 🕯️ Summary

The religious leadership of Vilgeismar, headquartered at the **Cathedral of Twelve Faces**. They are responsible for maintaining theological doctrine, organizing the perpetual city-wide hymnals, and managing the distribution of survival rations. They assert that the collective faith and spiritual unity of the city are the only things sustaining the Miracle.

---

### 🔍 Current Status

* **The High Lady**: Seraphine Vaelor, the symbolic center of the city, has almost entirely withdrawn from public view since the Miracle.
* **The Sisters**: They devote extended hours to prayer and silence within the restricted **Inner Cloister**.
* **Perpetual Hymn**: The Sisters maintain the "Constant Song," leading choral rehearsals morning and night that thread through every street in the city.

---

### ⚠️ Internal Tensions & Mysteries

* **Unnatural Endurance**: Many Sisters appear physically weakened by extreme malnutrition but continue their rigorous duties without collapse. Reports suggest some have ceased eating altogether.
* **Vivid Dreaming**: Members of the clergy report unusually intense and vivid dreams following extended hymnals.
* **Doctrinal Rigidity**: Public teaching increasingly emphasizes that suffering is a spiritual necessity and that the Miracle must be borne with humility rather than questioned.

---

### 🤝 Key Figures

* **High Lady Seraphine Vaelor**: The composed yet authoritative leader who interprets the Miracle as both a divine mercy and a heavy responsibility.
