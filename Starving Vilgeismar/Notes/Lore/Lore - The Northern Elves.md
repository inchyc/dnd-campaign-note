# ❄️ Lore: Northern Elves

### 🕯️ Summary

A distinct group of Elves originating from a cold, harsh, ice-carved plain far to the north. Unlike the more ethereal or forest-dwelling stereotypes, these Elves are defined by their survivalist nature and incredible martial skill.

---

### 🛠️ Cultural Attributes

* **Master Craftsmen**: Renowned as great smiths and craftsmen, capable of producing high-quality weapons and gear.

* **Environment**: Their homeland is characterized by mountains and plains carved by ice, fostering a resilient and hardy culture.

---

### ⚔️ Combat & Prowess

* **Natural Warriors**: They possess an extreme natural prowess in battle and are noted for being "extremely prowess" in their combat techniques.

* **Style**: Their fighting styles are likely refined and disciplined, potentially connected to the "educated classy" systems observed in some city fencers.

---
