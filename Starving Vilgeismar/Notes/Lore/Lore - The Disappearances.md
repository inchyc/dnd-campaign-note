# 🕵️ The Disappearances

### 🕯️ Summary

A string of residents have vanished from the Butcher’s Row district, including **Bernard** and **Albrecht**. These events do not appear to be standard kidnappings but are linked to specific social gatherings in the area.

---

### 🏮 Notable Clues

* **The "Parties"**: Local rumors point toward "parties" held in Butcher’s Row as the common denominator for the missing.

* **Witness Testimony**: Reports suggest the atmosphere surrounding these gatherings is "suspicious" or unnatural.
