# 👤 Lore: The Granary Phantoms

### 🕯️ Summary

Spectral entities encountered within the Granary Vaults. Unlike the mindless hunger of the Grain Monster, these phantoms exhibit highly disciplined combat behaviors. They appear to be "tethered" to a specific location or controller rather than being truly free-roaming spirits.

---

### ⚔️ Combat Profile

* **Martial Prowess**: Despite attacking with claws, their movement patterns and attack vectors suggest extensive training in weapons or unarmed combat.
* **Light Sensitivity**: Their physical presence and effectiveness seem significantly diminished in bright light.
* **Resilience**: They showed no reaction or vulnerability to the **Hymn of Death**, suggesting they are not "undead" or "corrupted" in the same way as the Grain Monster.

---

### 🧪 Arcane Nature: The Marionette Effect

* **The Strings**: Detect Magic revealed a sensation of "marionette strings" pulling taut and snapping just before the shadows faded.
* **Retreat Pattern**: Upon "death," the entities did not dissipate into the ether; instead, they rushed toward the back of the vault and presumably through the wall.
* **Magic Origin**: The energy felt "very old and very wild". While potentially related to hedge magic, the sophistication of their movement suggests this magic might be a cover for a more refined source.

---

### 🕵️ Theoretical Connections

* **Alignment**: It is currently unclear if the Phantoms are in league with the forces behind the Grain Monster. 
* **The "Strings" Theory**: The snapping strings suggest they are being remotely operated. This could link them to the "Notable Figure" traitor or a high-ranking caster within the city.
* **Hedge Magic vs. High Art**: While the Grain Monster used "Hedge Magic" (Outer Tribes), the Phantoms' fencing-like style suggests an educated or "classy" origin.
