# 🎭 The Hidden Face

The aspect that has no name and no image. The Church teaches that this face represents what mortals cannot understand. 

* **Ritual:** It is invoked only in the most solemn rituals. No icon depicts it; altars leave a blank space.
* **Theories:** Some theologians argue all miracles belong to the Hidden Face; others argue invoking it courts danger.
* **Symbol:** A blank space.
