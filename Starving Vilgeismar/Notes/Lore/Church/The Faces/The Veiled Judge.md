# 🎭 The Veiled Judge

The Lady as moral reckoning. She sees all acts without passion and measures them without haste.

* **Invocation:** Oaths are sworn in her name. 
* **Affiliation:** Magistrates favor this face.
* **Symbol:** Balanced scales behind a veil.
