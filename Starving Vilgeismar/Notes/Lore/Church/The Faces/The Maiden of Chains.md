# 🎭 The Maiden of Chains

The Lady as endurance in suffering. She represents resilience, captivity borne with dignity, and strength through restraint. 

* **Affiliation:** Prisoners and the besieged often cling to this face.
* **Symbol:** Broken shackles held together by light.
