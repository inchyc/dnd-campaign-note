# 🎼 The Unfamiliar Verse

### 🕯️ Summary

A specific stanza reportedly added to the **Hymn of Twelve Faces** exactly seven days ago, coinciding with the appearance of the Veil. While the Church maintains it is part of the divine protection, many congregants find the lyrics and melody jarringly unfamiliar.

---

### 🔍 Observations & Rumors

* **The Chronology**: The verse appeared the same night the outer gate was breached and the defenders faltered. 
* **The "Weight" of Sound**: Some believe this verse is what makes the morning bells feel "heavier" and the air feel "thick".
* **High Lady's Withdrawal**: The verse's introduction matches the period when High Lady Seraphine withdrew almost entirely into the Inner Cloister.
* **Dream Induction**: There is a persistent theory among the "Quiet Doubters" that this specific verse is causing the unusually vivid dreams and the unnatural fasting abilities of the Sisters.

---

### 🕵️ Investigation Leads

* **The Weeping Lady Connection**: Does the document the High Lady is seen reading in the "Omen" vision contain the lyrics to this verse?

* **Lyrical Drift**: No written record shows an alteration to the hymn, yet people *feel* it is different; is the magic itself rewriting the memories of the city?
