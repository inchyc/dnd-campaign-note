# 🎼 Hymn of Death

### 🕯️ Summary

A destructive resonant chant that facilitates decay and the releasing of physical form. It is the liturgical counterpoint to the Hymn of Birth and is associated with the Lady of Ash.

---

### ⚔️ Combat Application

* **Disintegration**: Successfully used to counter the Grain Monster's physical cohesion, causing it to fall apart into harmless rot.
* **Effectiveness**: It appears to be a direct "antidote" to manifestations created by corrupted life magic.

---

### 🧪 Sister's Mastery

* **Field Use**: Vex/Aewren has demonstrated the ability to perform this hymn under pressure to neutralize ritual threats.
* **Limitation**: It did not affect the Granary Phantoms, suggesting they are not "living manifestations" or traditional undead.
