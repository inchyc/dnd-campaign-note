# 🎼 Hymn of Birth

### 🕯️ Summary

A resonant chant associated with manifestation and growth. While traditionally used by the Church to invoke the Mother of Mercy for healing and childbirth, it has been observed being used in a corrupted form by Hedge Magic practitioners.

---

### 🔍 Tactical Observations

* **The Granary Ritual**: Used by the blue creature and the phantoms to "spawn" the Grain Monster from rot.
* **Resonance Frequency**: The chant causes physical matter to coalesce and take form.
* **Visual Cues**: When performed, it seemed to resonate with the "Hedge Magic" sigils (chicken skin and flowers) found in the vault.
