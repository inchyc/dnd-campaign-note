# 🎵 Lore: The Hymns of the Twelve Faces

> [!abstract] **Sonic Architecture**
> Vilgeismar is currently sustained by a perpetual cycle of chanting[cite: 3, 4]. These are not merely prayers; they function as a form of sonic architecture that maintains the **Veil** and actively influences the physical reality of the city[cite: 4].

### ⚠️ Supernatural Observations

* **The Constant Song**: The singing "can never stop," threading through the streets in a mournful, disciplined harmony. 
* **Resonance Mechanics**: The hymns work by vibrating the "essence" of an object; the **Hymn of Death** broke the physical cohesion of its target.
* **Hunger & Endurance**: Participation suppresses the physical sensation of hunger. The Sisters lead these 24/7, displaying an endurance that should be physically impossible without external aid.
* **Psychological Impact**: Extended exposure causes unusually vivid dreaming among the clergy and citizenry.

---

### 🕵️ Theoretical Ties

* **The "Hidden Face"**: Persistent rumors suggest a verse exists for a "thirteenth" or "hidden" face stricken from official records.
* **Enemy Corruption**: The Vanguard (Hedge Magic practitioners) may be using corrupted versions of these stanzas to facilitate their own summons.
* **Iron Throats**: The resonance of the morning bells and hymns now feels "heavier," as if the air itself has thickened.
