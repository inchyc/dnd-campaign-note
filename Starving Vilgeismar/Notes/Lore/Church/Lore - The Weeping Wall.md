# 🕯️ Lore: The Weeping Wall

The "Weeping Wall" refers to the inner perimeter of Vilgeismar’s fortifications where the Miracle’s veil is most concentrated. It is named for the emotional intensity and physical "weeping" (nosebleeds) experienced by those who linger too long in its shadow.

### 🧪 Known Effects

* **Emotional Intensity**: Those near the wall report sudden, overwhelming surges of emotion that have no clear external cause.
* **Sensory Distortion**: The iridescence of the veil shifts from pearl to dull silver and opaline, creating a disorienting visual effect.
* **The "Weight"**: Proximity to the wall often coincides with the feeling that the air has thickened, making breath and movement feel more deliberate.

### 🕵️ Theoretical Ties

* **Spiritual Awareness**: The Clergy officially attributes the physical symptoms (dizziness/nosebleeds) to "heightened spiritual awareness" rather than physical harm.
* **The Veil's Thickness**: The barrier is noted to be physically and magically thickest above the Gatehouse section of the wall.
