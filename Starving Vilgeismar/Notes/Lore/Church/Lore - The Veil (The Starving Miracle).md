# 📜 Lore: The Veil (The Starving Miracle)

Markdown


> [!abstract] **Visual Description** 
> A massive, shimmering dome of pale, iridescent light that encompasses the entirety of Vilgeismar. It shifts in color from a soft pearlescent white to a dull, bruised silver depending on the time of day.

### ✨ Known Properties

- **Physical Defense:** Projectiles and spells from the besieging army simply dissipate or "slide" off the surface of the dome.
    
- **Atmospheric Change:** The air inside the dome has become "metallic and sweet," and the sky above is perpetually hazy, blurring the sun and stars.
    
- **The Hum:** A low-frequency vibration can be felt in the teeth and bones of anyone standing within 50 feet of the perimeter.
    

### ⚠️ Supernatural Symptoms (The Cost)

- **The Weeping Wall:** Citizens living near the edge of the Veil report chronic nosebleeds, dizziness, and sudden bursts of uncontrollable emotion (grief or euphoria).
    
- **Hunger Suppression:** The closer one is to the center of the Veil's influence (the [[The Well]]), the less they feel the physical pangs of starvation.
    
- **Dreams:** Since the Veil appeared, residents have reported "shared" vivid dreams, often involving the [[The Cathedral]].
    

### 🔍 Investigation & Theories

- **The Seven-Day Link:** The Veil appeared exactly seven days ago, the same day the [[The Disappearances]] began.

- **The Hymn Connection:** It is maintained by the near-constant chanting of the Sisters of the Cloister. If the chanting stops, the Veil may flicker or fail.

- **The Unfamiliar Verse:** Rumors suggest the "Miracle" verse used to summon the Veil was never part of the traditional liturgy of the Twelve Faces.

- **The Vanguard**: It seems that part of [[The Vanguard]] that were assulting the city may have a foothold or a way into the city that we were not aware off. It also seem as though one of there leaders [[Harcon]] may be in the city soon or even in the city already.
