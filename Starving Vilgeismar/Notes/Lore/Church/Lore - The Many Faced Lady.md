### 🎼 Religious Practice

- **The Constant Hymn:** The city’s protection is believed to be sustained by the [[Lore - The Hymns of the Many Faced Lady]], which provide a perpetual cycle of prayer for each aspect.
    
- **The Miracle:** Theology holds that the **Hidden Face** is responsible for the iridescent veil, though this remains a point of intense scholarly debate.
    
- **Condition of Daily Life:** Devotion is expressed through discipline, endurance, and communal ritual rather than expectation of divine rescue.
