# ⚔️ Lore: The Vanguard

### 🕯️ Summary

The Vanguard is the primary assault force of the **Outer Tribes** currently laying siege to Vilgeismar. While the "Miracle" barrier has halted their physical advance, they have successfully infiltrated the city with specialized operatives to sabotage its internal infrastructure.

---

### 👥 Composition & Hierarchy

* **Commanders**: Led by figures like **Harcon**, described as a "Big One" and a powerful Orc commander.
* 
* **Racial Makeup**: Historically comprised of Orcs, but also utilizes specialized units from the **Goblins** and **Centaurs** for ritualistic and magical tasks.
* 
* **Tactical Specializations**: Employs "Hedge Magic" practitioners and ritual summoners to create environmental hazards (such as the Grain Monster).

---

### 🛡️ Involvement in the Siege

* **Initial Assault**: The Vanguard led the final assault on the **Gatehouse** before the Miracle appeared.
* 
* **Internal Operations**: They have transitioned from direct siege warfare to internal sabotage, focusing on destroying food supplies and morale.
* 
* **The "Big One"**: High-ranking members are believed to be hiding within the city districts, coordinating with agents like the blue "Hedge Magic" creature found in the Granary.
