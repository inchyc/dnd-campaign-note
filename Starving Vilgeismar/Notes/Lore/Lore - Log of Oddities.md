# 🌌 The Log of Oddities

> [!abstract] **Core Observation**
> The "Miracle" is not a static shield; it is an active environmental shift affecting the biology, psychology, and sensory reality of Vilgeismar.

---

## 🩸 Sensory & Physical Anomalies
* **The Weeping Wall Effect**: Citizens near the interior walls report dizziness, intense emotional shifts, and nosebleeds.
* **Altered Taste**: Water from [[TTRPG/D&D/Starving Vilgeismar/Notes/Locations/The Well of First Water|The Well of First Water]] tastes sweet or metallic.
* **Hunger Suppression**: Drinking the well water or participating in communal hymnals reduces the physical sensation of hunger.
* **The City's Scent**: A "sour tang of boiled leather" and a "metallic, faintly sweet" scent clings to everything.
* **Rain**: Water falling from the Veil almost like rain, is actually condensation and it is warm. 
* **Light Refraction**: The Sunlight refracts through the dome of the Veil. Seems it could be quite a significant effect and may be allowing [[Lore - Vampires]] to wander where they normally couldn't

---

## 🧠 Psychological & Spiritual Shifts
* **Vivid Dreaming**: Congregants and clergy report unusually intense dreams during constant hymnals.
* **Unnatural Endurance**: A minority of citizens and several [[Faction - The High Lady and the Sisters of the Cloister|Sisters]] have fasted for days beyond physical limits without collapsing.
* **The Unfamiliar Verse**: The final verse of the [[Lore - The Hymns of the Many Faced Lady|Hymn of Many Faced Lady]] feels "wrong" or new to congregants despite no written changes.
* **Eerie Calm**: An instinctual, slow reluctance observed in the weakest citizens of [[Beggar's Court]].

---

## 🏗️ The Internal Collapse (Location Risks)
* **The Cathedral**: The epicenter of the dreaming phenomenon.
* **Butcher's Row**: Whispers of disappearances among the vulnerable near orderly ration sites.
* **The Empty Granary**: Public suspicion of hoarding led to three merchant executions this month.

---

## 🌌 The Veil's Behavior
* **Visual Distortion**: Appears as heat distortion in early light; becomes an iridescent dome as the sun rises.
* **Shifting Colors**: Shifts from pearl to dull silver or opaline; it does not flicker.
* **Localized Density**: Thickest directly above the Gatehouse—the site of the final assault.
