# 📜 Lore: The Old Gods

> [!quote] “Gods die. And when they truly die they are unmourned and unremembered. Ideas are more difficult to kill than people, but they can be killed, in the end.”

## 🔍 Overview
For those of us from Vilgeismar, who thought of the [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady|The Many Faced Lady]] to be the only God, we have now been disabused of that notion.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
- There are along with what is colloquially know as The Old Gods also [[Lore - The Little Gods|The Little Gods]], [[Lore - The Great Vampire Lords|The Great Vampire Lords]], rumours of [[Lore - The Lost One|The Lost One]], [[Lore - The Mother of the Old Gods|The Mother of the Old Gods]], and possibly more that we have yet to learn about.
- The [[NPC - The Many Faced Lady|The Many Faced Lady]] and [[NPC - Dawn|Dawn]] are the only 2 of The Old Gods we know the name of.
- There seem to be 12 of The Old Gods and they seem to be siblings.
- The Little Gods also seem to be siblings of The Old Gods, though The Old Gods, seem to be the original 12 siblings.
- There seems to be some sort of weird power play between these siblings, all ruling over their various areas.
- The Old Gods seem to tend towards Egoism rather than the Altruism of The Little Gods.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> It seems that the machinations of The Little Gods (Mainly Demire thus far) have us working to some end involving The Old Gods who aren't ah exactly wanting to cooperate.
