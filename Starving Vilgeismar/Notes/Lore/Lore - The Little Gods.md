# 📜 Lore: The Little Gods

> [!quote] “Imagine for a moment that within your mind sits a grand council table, and around it gather a pantheon of gods.”

> 

## 🔍 Overview
For those of us from Vilgeismar, who thought of the [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady|The Many Faced Lady]] to be the only God, we have now been disabused of that notion.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* There are along with what is colloquially know as The Little Gods also [[Lore - The Old Gods|The Old Gods]], [[Lore - The Great Vampire Lords|The Great Vampire Lords]], [[Lore - The Mother of the Old Gods|The Mother of the Old Gods]], rumours of [[Lore - The Lost One|The Lost One]] and possibly more that we have yet to learn about.
* [[NPC - Demire AKA The Traveller|Demire]] also known of the Traveller is the one that first exposed us to this. He also happens to be the first of the Little Gods that we have met.
* Followers of Little Gods seem to have unique ways of so favour/fealty to them, like wearing different coloured patches of cloth for those that follow Demire.
* [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady|The Many Faced]] and [[Lore - Dawn|Dawn]] are examples of [[Lore - The Old Gods|The Old Gods]]. They seem to be a set of 12 original siblings. 
* Interactions so far seem to indicate that The Little Gods, while eccentric, are altruistic in general, we as our other integrations with The Old Gods thus far have indicated that they are less so.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> There seems to be a contention of sorts between The Old Gods, The Little Gods and The Great Vampire Lords.
