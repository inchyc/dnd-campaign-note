# 💧 The Well of the First Water

### 🕯️ Summary
The primary water source for the district has begun to exhibit physical and sensory changes. It is currently unknown if this is a byproduct of the **Grain Spoilage** or a separate act of sabotage.

---

### 🧪 Analysis
* **Sensory Profile**: The water has developed a distinct "sweet/metallic" taste.
* **Cross-Contamination**: This sensory profile is identical to the scent reported in the spoiled grain vaults, suggesting a unified source of corruption.

---

### ⚠️ Hypotheses
* **Spiritual/Theological**: A manifestation of the **Dead Harvest** ritual.
* **Alchemical**: Intentional poisoning by the "Notable Figure" traitor.
