# 📜 Lore: Vampires

> [!quote] * "The hunger of the belly is a simple thing, easily sated by bread. But the hunger of the blood... that is a void that no miracle can fill."* — Attributed to a heretical text in the Archives.

## 🔍 Overview
Are Vampires in Vilgeismar are a subject of concern and worthy of whispered suspicion? While the city starves under the siege, certain individuals among the nobility appear remarkably "well-nourished" and a lot of parties have been held by the nobles lately where people seem to be going in and not coming out. Is there a predatory class of undead thriving in the shadows of the [[Lore - The Veil (The Starving Miracle)|The Veil]].

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Unlike the skeletal, sharp-featured citizens of the city, suspected vampires maintain a healthy, even robust, physical appearance. They often carry a "metallic, faintly sweet" scent, similar to the scent noted in [[Lore - Log of Oddities]].
* **The Refraction Effect:** Vex is theorizing the dome of [[Lore - The Veil (The Starving Miracle)|The Veil]] creates a "false day" or filtered sunlight. This may be allowing vampires to wander the streets during hours that would normally be fatal, albeit perhaps with a reduced presence.
* **Silver Sensitivity:** There is a known aversion to silver in myth and lore. High-ranking individuals like [[NPC - Lord Henrik Vaust]] have been observed using specialized "light detection" methods to screen visitor weapons. Is it to check if they have been silvered.
* **Public Perception:** To the common citizen, the idea of vampires is a nightmare of the old world, it is of myth. However, with these revelations it seems that concerns of those among the [[Faction - The Quiet Doubters]],  that the "nourished" status of the elite is seen as a sign of literal parasitism. May hold more truth than any of us could have know

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The presence of vampires within the [[The Noble Houses]] creates a profound risk to the city's stability. If the "Saviours of Vilgeismar" are actually its predators, the moral and social fabric of the city could collapse faster than the siege can break it. Furthermore, the thrum of necromancy associated with these beings suggests a deep corruption that may be linked to the "Miracle" itself.
