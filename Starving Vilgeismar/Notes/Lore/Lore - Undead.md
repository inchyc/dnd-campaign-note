# 📜 Lore: Lore - Undead

> [!quote] “The dead don’t walk. Except, sometimes, when they do.”

## 🔍 Overview

To the general population of Vilgeismar, the thought of the living dead was a fairy tale or a story told to scare you as a child. Little did they know that they had one of the most deadly kind walking amongst their most respected residents.


---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* Creatures/Humanoids thought to have past through the mortal veil that seem to be capable of still existing and acting long past when they should.
* Seems to be a few variations. For Example see [[Lore - Vampires]] and [[NPC - Lord Henrik Vaust]].
* Silver seems to be a weakness of at least Vampires, one would presume that Silver would affect any other types.
* Inconclusive if healing magic works for them or against them.
* They seem particularly adverse to "Divine Magic" whatever that happens to be now after recent revelations. See [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]], [[Lore - The Little Gods]], [[Lore - The Old Gods]]
