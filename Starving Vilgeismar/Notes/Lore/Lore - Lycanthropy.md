## 🔍 Overview
Lycanthropy in Vilgeismar is a whispered affliction, often associated with the shifting shadows and the growing instability within the city. While rare, recent events suggest a more organized or pervasive presence than previously suspected.

* **Sensory Details:** Shifting forms, animalistic aggression, and a notable vulnerability to silver.
* **Public Perception:** Fear and superstition. Most citizens treat it as a curse or a sign of divine disfavor.
