# 📜 The Rotting Harvest

### 🕯️ Summary

The grain vaults of Vilgeismar were infested by a "Grain Monster," a physical manifestation of the spoilage affecting the city’s food supply. This infestation was not merely natural decay but linked to ritualistic or spiritual corruption.

---

### 🎵 The Hymn of Death

The entity was defeated through the performance of the **Hymn of Death**.

* **Mechanical Effect**: The hymn resonated with the corruption, neutralizing the monster’s physical form.

* **Spiritual Context**: It represents a direct counter to the "growth" or "harvest" theme of the corruption

---

### 🕵️ Observations & Evidence

* **Metallic Scent**: The spoiled grain carries a sweet/metallic scent, matching the water in the city wells. - ammonium scent
* **"Grain Monster"** was a ritual summon tied to "Outer Tribe" hedge magic, specifically linked to Goblins or Centaurs. Ritual sigils along with flowers from outside the region wrapped in chicken skin along with a string ammonium smell.

* **The Bee Link**: [[Valcara]] provided the information about the source of the magic and managed to summon the goblin back to us. She also apparently really doesn't like Goblins, we allowed her to blow it up. Likely a good source for more information.

* **Cultist Involvement**?: Though the monster is dead, we have a lead, an Orc named Harcon. According to [[Valcara]] apparently there a very few hedge mages in or around the city. Possibility of cult?
