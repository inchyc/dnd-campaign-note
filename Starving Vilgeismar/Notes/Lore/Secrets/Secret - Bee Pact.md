# 🌑 Secret: The Bee Pact


> [!caution] **The Whispered Truth** High Lady Seraphine has a secret agreement with the beekeeper Valcara to dispose of the starved dead by "harvesting" them in the gardens.

### 🔍 Evidence & Corroboration

- **Primary Source:** [[Valcara]] explicitly stated she has an agreement with the High Lady.

- **Physical Evidence:** The party discovered poorly buried bodies in the gardens being used by bees for pollination.

- **Supporting Clues:** The High Lady has confirmed there is an agreement in place.


### 🧪 Potential Consequences

- **If True:** It confirms the High Lady is willing to desecrate the dead to sustain the city's (or the bees') resources.

- **If Publicized:** Total loss of faith from the citizens; potential riots in Beggar's Court.
