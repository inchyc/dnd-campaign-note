# 🌑 Secret: The Omen (Vision of the Falling Lady)

Markdown


> [!abstract] **The Vision** 
> The High Lady sits alone in a quiet chamber of the Cathedral, the constant thrum of the city's hymnals muffled by the thick stone. She is weeping, her face buried in a worn piece of parchment. Before she can look up, a shadow crosses the room, and she is slain._

### 🔍 Key Details & Observations

- **The Location:** Specifically identified as a private chamber within [[The Cathedral of Twelve Faces]].
    
- **The Item:** She was fixated on a **parchment**. It appeared significant enough to cause her visible distress/grief.
    
- **The State of the Lady:** She was alone and vulnerable, lacking her usual "furious" or authoritative public persona.
    
- **The Execution:** The vision implies a targeted assassination rather than a public execution.
    

### 🧩 Connections & Corroboration

- **The Traitor Thread:** This vision directly aligns with the rumors from [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]] that [[Guard Captain Roderic Hale]] or another "notable figure" intends to remove her.
    
- **The High Lady's Disposition:** Her recent shift to being "furious" and demanding public confessions might be a defensive reaction to the contents of that parchment or a fear of this exact betrayal.
    

### 🧪 Potential Meanings

1. **The Parchment as Proof:** The parchment might contain proof of the [[Secret - Bee Pact]] or evidence that the "Miracle" has a dark cost.
    
2. **The Assassin:** If the shadow in the dream matches the build of anyone in the [[Codex - Archive#👥 THE VILGEISMAR CODEX (NPCs)]], they become the prime suspect for the Traitor.
    
3. **Preventability:** Omens in this world are often warnings of what _will_ happen if the current path remains unchanged.
