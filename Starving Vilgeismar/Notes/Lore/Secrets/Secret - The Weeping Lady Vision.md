# 👁️ Secret: The Weeping Lady Vision

A vivid and unsettling vision experienced exactly seven days after the appearance of the Miracle. Unlike a standard dream, this vision felt "too real" and carried a sense of impending tragedy for the city's leadership.

### 📜 The Vision Sequence

1. **The Setting**: High Lady Seraphine Vaelor is seen alone, isolated from her usual council and the Sisters of the Cloister.
2. **The Catalyst**: She is reading a specific document or parchment that causes her to weep.
3. **The Assassination**: A figure approaches her with a knife in hand. She is stabbed and slain.
4. **The Aftermath**: As her blood drips, a collective screaming begins, echoing through the vision.

### 🕵️ Critical Questions

* **The Document**: What information was contained in the parchment that caused the High Lady to weep before her death?
* **The Figure**: Is the assassin a member of the "Quiet Doubters," a traitor within the Noble Houses, or an agent of the Vanguard like Harcon?
* **The Scream**: Does the screaming signify the failure of the Veil, or the beginning of a transformation within the city?
