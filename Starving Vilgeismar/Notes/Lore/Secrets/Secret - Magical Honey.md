# 🍯 Secret - Magical Honey

### 🕯️ Summary
The "Honey" in Vilgeismar is not merely a food source but a potent alchemical or divine substance used to sustain the city's elite and its defenders. While it is presented as a gift of the Miracle, its true origins are far more grim.

---

### 🌑 The Whispered Truth: The Dead Harvest
* **The Source**: Evidence suggests the High Lady is using the corpses of the dead to feed bees, creating a cycle of "Dead Harvest" to generate this sustenance[cite: 3].
* **The Ritual**: This process is likely linked to the **Bee Pact** and the spiritual corruption seen in the grain vaults[cite: 3].
* **The Miracle's Mask**: While the High Lady presents the Honey as a "Mercy" from the Many-Faced Lady, it is a pragmatic solution to the famine fueled by the city's own casualties[cite: 2].

---

### 🧪 Varieties & Effects
The party has acquired several specialized "honeys," primarily provided by **Valcara**[cite: 1]:
* **Healing Honey (Greater)**: Potent restorative used for severe injuries.
* **Speed Honey**: Enhances physical reflexes and movement.
* **Mystery Honey**: A variant with unknown or shifting effects.

---

### ⚠️ Observations
* **Nutritional Suppression**: Consumption of communal substances (like well water or potentially this honey) appears to temporarily reduce the physical sensation of hunger.
* **Clerical Endurance**: Several Sisters of the Cloister have begun fasting for days beyond normal physical limits without collapsing, likely supported by this substance.
