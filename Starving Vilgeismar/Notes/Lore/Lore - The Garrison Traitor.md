# 🗡️ Lore: The Garrison Traitor

### 🕯️ Summary
Internal intelligence suggests a "notable figure" within the city’s defense or administration is operating as a traitor. The primary focus of suspicion is currently on the military leadership, specifically regarding a potential coup against the Cloister.

---

### 🌑 The Whispered Truth
**Captain Roderic Hale** intends to institute martial law to forcibly remove **High Lady Seraphine** from power. He reportedly feels the Garrison’s sacrifice has been rendered "irrelevant" by the Miracle and fears internal desperation is as dangerous as the enemy army.

---

### ⚖️ Evidence & Corroboration
* **Primary Source**: Garron Thrice-Garrotted claimed this during negotiations in Butcher's Row.
* **Motive**: Likely linked to the ongoing siege and the spiritual corruption affecting the city's food and water. Hale is pragmatic and may view the current leadership as incompetent in the face of these threats.
* **Contradictions**: Garron is a crime lord with a vested interest in destabilizing the city’s official leadership; his testimony may be a calculated fabrication.

---

### 🧪 Potential Consequences
* **If True**: A civil war within the walls between the Garrison and the Cloister.
* **If Publicized**: Could cause the High Lady to purge the military, leaving the walls undefended against the external siege.

---

### 🕵️ Investigation Leads
* **The "Henrick" Connection**: Look into the **Lord Henrick** mentioned by Dieta, who supposedly has a vested interest in the church.
* **Garrison Demeanor**: Monitor Hale and his officers for signs of shifting loyalty or preparation for martial law.

---

### 👥 Primary Suspects
* **Captain Hale**: Commander of the Garrison.
