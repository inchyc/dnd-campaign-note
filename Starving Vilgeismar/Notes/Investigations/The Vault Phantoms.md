# 🕵️ Investigation: The Vault Phantoms

> [!abstract] **Current Objective**
> Determine if the spectral entities in the vault are independent threats or tools of a specific faction.

### 🔍 Active Leads
- [ ] **The "Strings" Mechanism**: Consult Valcara regarding the "snapping strings" sensation. Is this a known necromantic tether or something more "wild"?
- [ ] **The Fencing Connection**: Cross-reference the "disciplined combat moves" of the phantoms with the "educated classy" fencing style noted on the granary ceiling.
- [ ] **Structural Damage**: Inspect the back wall of the Granary vault where the phantoms disappeared to see if there is a physical passage or a magical anchor.

### 📝 Findings & Intel
* **The Marionette Effect**: Unlike the Grain Monster, these entities appeared to be controlled via invisible strings that snapped upon their defeat.
* **Immunity to Hymns**: The phantoms were unaffected by the **Hymn of Death**, suggesting they are not typical manifestations of the "Dead Harvest" or the grain rot.
* **Skillset**: Their movements suggest they were skilled combatants in life, or are being possessed/simulated by a master of martial arts.
