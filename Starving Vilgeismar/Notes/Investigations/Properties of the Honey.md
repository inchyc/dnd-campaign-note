# 🕵️ Investigation: Properties of the Honey

> [!abstract] **Current Objective**
> Decipher the alchemical composition of the "Mystery Honey" and monitor the party for long-term effects of consumption.

### 🔍 Active Leads

- [ ] **Mystery Honey Analysis**: Consult Valcara or utilize a controlled test to determine the specific mechanical benefit (or risk) of the "Mystery Honey".

- [ ] **Sensation Monitoring**: Observe if consumption of the honey causes the same "suppression of hunger" noted in those who drink from the Well of First Water.

- [ ] **Withdrawal/Side Effects**: Monitor the party for symptoms like the "nosebleeds and dizziness" associated with the Veil, to see if the honey is a localized anchor for the Miracle.

### 📝 Findings & Intel

* **Current Stock**: The party currently holds 3x Healing Honey (Greater), 2x Speed Honey, and 2x Mystery Honey.

* **Acquisition**: These items were provided by Valcara as a reward/utility for the investigation at the Granary.

* **The High Lady Connection**: High Lady Seraphine presents these as a "Mercy," but they are likely the end-product of the **Bee Pact** (feeding the dead to the hives).
