# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-11-04

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party journeyed into the increasingly cold Elven territory, leaving their caravan behind. Vex experienced a magical transformation, her skin flaking like ash before healing, a sensation reminiscent of Demire and the Many-Faced Lady's magic. Upon reaching the Elven Glacier, they discovered a unique society where elves freeze portions of their population and practice open relationships, with monogamy reserved for royalty. They were greeted by Captain Eirndoor and encountered an Elven Prince, who was later joined by the enigmatic King Valefare the Winter Crowned, a pale figure with dark eyes, webbed fingers, and a coral crown. The King has forbidden the party from leaving until he speaks with them about Demire.

In the Hall of Ancestors, they met OldMirivale, the Elven Archivist, who revealed that Colm's 'Vampire Sword' is one of the powerful 'Great Fangs' wielded by ancient Vampire Lords who once attempted continental conquest. She also called Lord Henrik Vaust a 'rat,' suggested Colm research 'Dawn,' and questioned Ruik about being 'Echo Touched.' The party also acquired a 'Cloudy Glass Eye' and a 'Ring of Little Deception.' Key revelations included Vilgeismar's young age (60 years) and the existence of a 'Teaweird' on Demire's boat, The Eternal Wake.
