# 📍 Hall of Ancestors

> [!quote] **Atmosphere**
> A solemn and ancient hall, filled with the carved images of past Elven rulers, echoing with history and forgotten tales.

### 🔍 Points of Interest
*   **Pillars of Rulers:** Five massive pillars, each carved with the likeness of an old Elven ruler. One pillar, depicting [[Lore - Elandra Last Daughter of the Deep]], has been badly defaced.
*   **Archivist's Domain:** The primary residence and workplace of [[NPC - OldMirivale (Archivist)|OldMirivale]], the Elven Archivist.

### 👥 Local Inhabitants
*   **Key NPC:** [[NPC - OldMirivale (Archivist)|OldMirivale]], [[Guard Captain Roderic Hale]] (currently staying here)
*   **Crowd Mood:** Quiet and reverent, befitting a place of historical significance.
