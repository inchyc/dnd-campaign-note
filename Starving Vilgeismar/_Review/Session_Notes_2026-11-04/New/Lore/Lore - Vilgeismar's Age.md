# 📜 Lore: Vilgeismar's Age

> [!quote] *"A city built on fresh foundations, yet already burdened by ancient woes."*

## 🔍 Overview
Contrary to its seemingly established presence, the city of Vilgeismar has only existed for a relatively short period of 60 years, a fact that may hold significance regarding its current predicament.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Recent Founding:** Its young age suggests that the city's history is not as deep as many might assume, potentially making it more vulnerable to ancient forces or less rooted in the land.
*   **Context for the Miracle:** The short history might provide context for why the 'Starving Miracle' manifested over Vilgeismar specifically.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> A city with such a short history might lack the deep-seated magical or spiritual protections that older settlements possess, making it more susceptible to external threats or internal corruption.
