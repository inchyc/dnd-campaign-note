# 📜 Lore: Vampires' Conquest

> [!quote] *"The hunger of the blood, once sought to engulf all lands."*

## 🔍 Overview
In ancient times, the [[Lore - The Great Vampire Lords|Great Vampire Lords]], wielding powerful artifacts like the [[Lore - The Great Fangs|Great Fangs]], attempted a widespread conquest of the continent.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Historical Event:** A significant historical period where vampires were a dominant, aggressive force.
*   **Tools of Conquest:** The [[Lore - The Great Fangs|Great Fangs]] were instrumental in their military campaigns.
*   **Elven Knowledge:** The Elven Archivist, [[NPC - OldMirivale (Archivist)|OldMirivale]], possesses knowledge of this event, suggesting it is a well-documented or remembered part of ancient history.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The rediscovery of the Great Fangs or the resurgence of powerful vampires could lead to a repeat of this devastating historical conflict.
