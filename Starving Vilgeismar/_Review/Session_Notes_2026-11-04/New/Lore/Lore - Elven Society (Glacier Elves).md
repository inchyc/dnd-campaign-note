# 📜 Lore: Elven Society (Glacier Elves)

> [!quote] *"We preserve our past to ensure our future, even if it means pausing life itself."*

## 🔍 Overview
The Elves of the Glacier territory have developed unique societal structures and practices to adapt to their environment and extended lifespans, including methods of population preservation and distinct relationship norms.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Population Freezing:** A significant portion of the Elven population is periodically frozen and defrosted as needed, suggesting a method of managing resources or preserving knowledge across vast spans of time.
*   **Pods (Open Relationships):** Elven society generally practices open relationships, referred to as "pods," where individuals may have multiple spouses, such as [[NPC - Captain Eirndoor]] and his spouses [[NPC - Gurtrout]], [[NPC - Lorin]], and [[NPC - Aurtin]].
*   **Royal Monogamy:** Uniquely, Elven royalty retains the right to monogamy, setting them apart from the general populace.
*   **"Smallings"**: Elves refer to shorter-lived races, such as humans, as "Smallings."
