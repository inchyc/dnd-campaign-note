# 📜 Lore: Dawn's Hammer

> [!quote] *"A weapon of creation, or perhaps, of ultimate destruction."*

## 🔍 Overview
[[PC Notes/Ruik|Ruik]] is in possession of an artifact known as Dawn's Hammer. Its connection to [[Lore - Dawn|Dawn]] and its full capabilities are unknown, but it is implied to have significant and potentially dangerous implications.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Possession:** Currently wielded by [[PC Notes/Ruik|Ruik]].
*   **Connection to Dawn:** The name strongly links it to [[Lore - Dawn|Dawn]], one of the [[Lore - The Old Gods|Old Gods]], suggesting divine or primordial power.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The presence of Dawn's Hammer, especially in the hands of someone potentially [[Lore - Echo Touched|Echo Touched]] like [[PC Notes/Ruik|Ruik]], could lead to catastrophic events, particularly if [[PC Notes/Colm|Colm]] is indeed [[Lore - Dawn|Dawn]] reborn. The Archivist hinted it "will likely end badly."
