# 📜 Lore: Echo Touched

> [!quote] *"To hear the whispers of what was, and what might yet be."*

## 🔍 Overview
"Echo Touched" is a mysterious condition or ability, hinted at by the Elven Archivist, [[NPC - OldMirivale (Archivist)|OldMirivale]], when she questioned [[PC Notes/Ruik|Ruik]] about it. Its nature and implications are currently unknown.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Elven Terminology:** The term originates from Elven lore, suggesting it might be an ancient or rare phenomenon.
*   **Potential Abilities:** The name implies a connection to echoes, memories, or perhaps even precognition or postcognition.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> Being "Echo Touched" could grant powerful, but potentially dangerous or sanity-straining, insights into the past or future, or make one a target for those seeking such knowledge.
