# 📜 Lore: DrewDrops

> [!quote] *"The deep earth's tears, traded for the surface's desires."*

## 🔍 Overview
DrewDrops are the primary form of currency used by the Elves of the Glacier territory, found exclusively in deep caves.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Source:** Mined or collected from deep caves, suggesting a rare and valuable resource.
*   **Elven Currency:** Serves as the standard medium of exchange within Elven society.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The scarcity of DrewDrops, being found only in deep caves, could lead to economic instability or dangerous mining expeditions.
