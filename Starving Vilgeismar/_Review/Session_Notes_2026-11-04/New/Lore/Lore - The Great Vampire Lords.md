# 📜 Lore: The Great Vampire Lords

> [!quote] *"Their reign was a shadow cast across the continent, fueled by blood and ambition."*

## 🔍 Overview
The Great Vampire Lords were ancient and powerful figures who once held significant sway, attempting to conquer the continent. They were the original wielders of the formidable [[Lore - The Great Fangs|Great Fangs]].

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Historical Power:** These lords were a dominant force in ancient history, leading widespread campaigns of conquest.
*   **Artifact Wielders:** They were associated with and wielded the [[Lore - The Great Fangs|Great Fangs]], powerful enchanted swords.
*   **Elven Perspective:** The Elven Archivist, [[NPC - OldMirivale (Archivist)|OldMirivale]], holds knowledge of their history, including their failed conquest attempts, and views figures like [[NPC - Lord Henrik Vaust|Count Vaust]] with disdain, implying a connection or lineage.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The legacy of the Great Vampire Lords, and the potential for their return or the rise of new figures wielding their power, represents a significant threat to the continent.
