# 📜 Lore: Smallings

> [!quote] *"A fleeting spark, quickly extinguished, yet sometimes burning bright."*

## 🔍 Overview
"Smallings" is a term used by the Elves of the Glacier territory to refer to shorter-lived races, such as humans, highlighting their perception of other races' comparatively brief lifespans.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Cultural Term:** Reflects the Elven perspective on longevity and the transient nature of other races.
*   **Perception:** Implies a sense of ancient wisdom or perhaps condescension from the long-lived Elves towards "Smallings."

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> This terminology could lead to misunderstandings or cultural clashes between Elves and other races, potentially fostering resentment or a sense of inferiority.
