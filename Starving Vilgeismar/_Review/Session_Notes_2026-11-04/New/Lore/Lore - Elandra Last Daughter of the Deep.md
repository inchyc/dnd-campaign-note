# 📜 Lore: Elandra Last Daughter of the Deep

> [!quote] *"A name etched in stone, then violently erased from memory."*

## 🔍 Overview
Elandra Last Daughter of the Deep was an ancient Elven ruler whose carved image in the [[Location - Hall of Ancestors|Hall of Ancestors]] has been severely defaced, suggesting a significant and perhaps controversial past.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Defaced Monument:** Her pillar in the Hall of Ancestors is the only one among five to be badly defaced, indicating a deliberate act of historical erasure or condemnation.
*   **Title:** The title "Last Daughter of the Deep" hints at a connection to deep-sea origins or a lineage that ended with her.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The defacement suggests a dark or forbidden chapter in Elven history, and uncovering the truth behind it could reveal dangerous secrets or reignite old conflicts.
