# 📜 Lore: The Great Fangs

> [!quote] *"Blades born of hunger, wielded by those who sought to consume the world."*

## 🔍 Overview
The Great Fangs are a collection of powerful, enchanted swords, notably including [[PC Notes/Colm|Colm]]'s [[staged:Item - Vampire Rapier|Vampire Rapier]]. These weapons were bestowed upon the [[Lore - The Great Vampire Lords|Great Vampire Lords]] in ancient times, imbued with immense power.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Ancient Power:** Each Great Fang is enchanted with significant power, reflecting the might of the Vampire Lords who once wielded them.
*   **Vampiric Connection:** These blades are intrinsically linked to the history and ambitions of the [[Lore - The Great Vampire Lords|Great Vampire Lords]], who used them in their attempts to conquer the continent.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The existence and rediscovery of these weapons could reignite ancient conflicts or empower new vampiric threats, especially given their history of continental conquest.
