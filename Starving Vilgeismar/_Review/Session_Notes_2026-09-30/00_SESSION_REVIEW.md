# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-09-30

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party found themselves in a new, war-torn region outside Vilgeismar, now under the control of wererats. They successfully saved a group of humans by guiding them to the wererat's underground lair, during which their gnome companion, Rooprecht, was transformed into a wererat. Investigating recent disappearances, the party discovered an ancient, buried temple dedicated to a forgotten Patriarch, possibly a divine counterpart to the Many-Faced Lady. Inside, they found mummified infant bodies, a room with twelve cribs (eleven containing preserved bodies), and fought animated Rotlings. Colm had a disturbing encounter with a spirit from one of the cribs, suggesting it acts as a portal. Everan experienced a vision revealing the Many-Faced Lady has twelve divine children/siblings, one of whom was excluded. The party also found a library with celestial texts, deciphering inscriptions about life, death, and rebirth, and murals depicting a figure named Dorn and a mace called 'Cradlebreaker'. They recovered a magical cloudy glass eye and a ring of minor illusion.
