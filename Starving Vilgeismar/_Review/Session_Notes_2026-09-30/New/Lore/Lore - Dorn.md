# 📜 Lore: Dorn

> [!quote] *"The old must die to birth the new."

## 🔍 Overview
A figure depicted in murals within [[Location - The Patriarch's Temple]], shown striking a husky and associated with the cycle of death, rebirth, and life. Possibly a child or aspect of [[Lore - The Patriarch]].

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Sensory Details:** Depicted in murals within the temple, suggesting a strong, active presence in the Patriarch's theology.
*   **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten.
*   **Theological Significance:** Appears to be a significant figure in the Patriarch's faith, possibly a god of cycles, a warrior deity, or a divine child/sibling of the Patriarch.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The teachings associated with Dorn, particularly the commandment "The old must die to birth the new," could be interpreted in ways that challenge the current leadership and stability of Vilgeismar.
