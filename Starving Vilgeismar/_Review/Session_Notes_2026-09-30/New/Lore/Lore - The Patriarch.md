# 📜 Lore: The Patriarch

> [!quote] *"From blood comes life, from life comes death, from death, the next."

## 🔍 Overview
A newly discovered divine figure, possibly an opposite or equal to [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]], associated with a hidden, ancient temple. Engravings of a Man and a child, with maces scratched out, suggest a complex history.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Sensory Details:** The temple dedicated to the Patriarch feels ancient and powerful, with mummified remains and preserved bodies suggesting a connection to life and death cycles.
*   **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten or suppressed by the dominant faith of the Many-Faced Lady.
*   **Theological Significance:** Represents a counter-pantheon or a lost aspect of the divine, potentially challenging the established theology of the Many-Faced Lady. Associated with 12 divine children/siblings, one of whom was excluded and mocked by the others.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The discovery of this forgotten deity and its associated lore could destabilize the city's faith and understanding of the 'Miracle'.
