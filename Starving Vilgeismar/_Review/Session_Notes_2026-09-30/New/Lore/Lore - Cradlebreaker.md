# 📜 Lore: Cradlebreaker

> [!quote] *"Mace is named cradle breaker. One hand life, the other death."

## 🔍 Overview
The name of a mace, associated with the second commandment found in [[Location - The Patriarch's Temple]]: "One hand life, the other death." Its images were deliberately scratched out from engravings within the temple, suggesting its significance was suppressed.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Sensory Details:** Depicted as a mace in engravings, embodying a duality of creation and destruction.
*   **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten or actively suppressed due to its potentially dangerous theological implications.
*   **Theological Significance:** A powerful artifact or concept central to the Patriarch's faith, embodying the duality of life and death, and possibly linked to the cycle of "the old must die to birth the new."

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The concept of "Cradlebreaker" and its associated philosophy could be highly destructive if misinterpreted or wielded by those seeking to hasten societal collapse.
