# 📍 The Patriarch's Temple

> [!quote] **Atmosphere**
> Ancient, hidden, and eerily preserved, with a sense of forgotten power.

### 🔍 Points of Interest
*   **The Landmark:** An ancient temple, seemingly buried and forgotten, dedicated to a figure known as the Patriarch.
*   **Supernatural Signs:** Mummified infant bodies, preserved bodies of varying ages in twelve cribs (one empty), animated Rotlings, and a portal-like crib that caused a spiritual invasion.
*   **Recent Activity:** The party investigated disappearances, fought Rotlings, and Colm had a disturbing spiritual encounter. Celestial texts, murals depicting a Woman with child, [[Lore - Dorn]] striking a husky, and the cycle of death, rebirth, and life were discovered. Inscriptions and commandments were found, along with bowls for Blood, Ash, and Water.

### 👥 Local Inhabitants
*   **Key NPC:** None (currently)
*   **Crowd Mood:** Eerie silence, disturbed only by the party's presence and the Rotlings.

### 🕸️ Leads & Oddities
- [ ] **The Portal-Cribs:** Investigate the nature and purpose of the cribs, especially the one acting as a portal.
- [ ] **The Patriarch's Identity:** Uncover more about [[Lore - The Patriarch]] and their relationship to [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]].
