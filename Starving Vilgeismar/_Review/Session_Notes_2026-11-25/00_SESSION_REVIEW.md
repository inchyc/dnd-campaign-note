# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-11-25

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party ventured beneath the Royal Gardens into a newly discovered glacial complex. They navigated collapsing tunnels, found bookshelves inscribed with an unknown ancient language, and rescued four prisoners (Brum, Garic, Burta, Nor) from a 'Full Moon Room.' During the rescue, Everan's hands glowed with mysterious red energy, seemingly acting on their own. Further into the complex, they discovered an aqueduct ('Crescent Moon Room') filled with rotting elven bodies, suggesting chaotic magic at play. The deepest chamber, the 'Old Queen's Tomb,' revealed an entombed ancient queen, who was confirmed by Demire (the Cat Demi-God, found hiding there) to be his sister. As the party prepared to leave, Demire, visibly terrified, warned them of a 'hero thing' and fled, just as an 8-foot tall, antlered being with blue eyes appeared in the doorway.
