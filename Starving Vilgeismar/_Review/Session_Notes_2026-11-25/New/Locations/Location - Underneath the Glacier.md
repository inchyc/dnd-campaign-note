# 📍 Underneath the Glacier

> [!quote] **Atmosphere**
> A network of ancient, collapsing tunnels and rooms beneath Vilgeismar, accessed via [[The Gardens]]. The air is cold and carries the scent of ancient stone and decay.

### 🔍 Points of Interest
* **The Landmark:** Collapsing tunnels, rooms with ancient languages, cells, an aqueduct, and an ancient tomb.
* **Supernatural Signs:** Presence of [[Demire AKA The Traveller]], ancient magic, rotting elven bodies, an [[Antlered Being]].
* **Recent Activity:** Party exploration, rescue of prisoners, encounter with Demire and the Antlered Being.

### 👥 Local Inhabitants
* **Key NPC:** [[Demire AKA The Traveller]] (briefly), [[The Old Queen]] (entombed), [[Brum]], [[Garic]], [[Burta]], [[Nor]] (prisoners).
* **Crowd Mood:** None (isolated location).
