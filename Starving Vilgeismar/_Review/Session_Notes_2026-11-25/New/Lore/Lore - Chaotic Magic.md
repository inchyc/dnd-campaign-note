# 📜 Lore: Chaotic Magic

> [!quote] *"When the threads of power fray, reality itself begins to unravel."

## 🔍 Overview
A state where magical energies become untangled, unpredictable, and potentially destructive, leading to unintended and often harmful consequences. This phenomenon is suspected to be responsible for the rotting elven bodies found in the aqueduct beneath the Glacier.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Observed as rotting elven bodies in water, implying a corrupting or destructive effect.
* **Public Perception:** Likely unknown or attributed to curses/blight if observed.
* **Theological Significance:** Could be seen as a sign of divine displeasure or a breakdown of the natural order.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> Uncontrolled chaotic magic poses a direct threat to life and stability, potentially causing widespread decay or unpredictable magical events.
