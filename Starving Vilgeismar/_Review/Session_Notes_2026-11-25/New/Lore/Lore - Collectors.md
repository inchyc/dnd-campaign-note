# 📜 Lore: Collectors

> [!quote] *"The Many-Faced Lady, in her infinite wisdom, gifted her Gods with servants to gather, keep, seek, and warden the truths of the world."

## 🔍 Overview
"Collectors" are a class of beings or entities, originally gifted by the [[The Many Faced Lady]] to the Gods. They come in various shapes and sizes, each with a specific purpose: Gathers, Keepers, Seekers, and Wardens.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Unknown, but they are associated with masks (e.g., [[Collector Mask]]).
* **Public Perception:** Likely unknown to the general populace, possibly a secret of the Church or ancient lore.
* **Theological Significance:** Directly linked to [[The Many Faced Lady]] and the Gods, suggesting a divine origin and purpose.
