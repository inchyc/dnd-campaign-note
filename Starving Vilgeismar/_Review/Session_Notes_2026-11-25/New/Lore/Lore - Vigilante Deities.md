# 📜 Lore: Vigilante Deities

> [!quote] *"Not all gods demand worship; some merely demand justice."

## 🔍 Overview
A concept describing minor deities or powerful entities who actively intervene to correct injustices, operating outside the traditional pantheon or established religious structures. [[Pikkir]] is identified as one such deity.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Manifestations or interventions are likely subtle or targeted.
* **Public Perception:** Likely seen as folk heroes, spirits of justice, or perhaps even dangerous vigilantes by authorities.
* **Theological Significance:** Challenges the passive or distant nature often attributed to [[The Many Faced Lady]] and her Twelve Faces, aligning more with the concept of [[The Little Gods]].

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The existence of such deities could undermine the authority of the Church or inspire rebellion against perceived injustices, potentially leading to chaos.
