# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-10-07

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party delved deeper into The Patriarch's Temple, confronting an avatar of Dawn, one of the Old Gods, situated within a ritualistic cradle. During the intense combat, Aewren suffered a fatal head injury, while Colm, already under a mysterious influence, found himself healed by his Vampire Rapier when striking the avatar. Demire, The Traveller, made a timely appearance, confirming the entity was merely an avatar of Dawn. He then performed a powerful ritual in the cradle, utilizing Dawn's hand and the blood of Vex, Vola, and Ruik, to successfully revive Aewren. Demire also provided crucial lore, confirming Colm's undead status was inflicted by 'The Lost Sibling' and clarifying Rooprecht's true nature as a wererat. He further hinted at the nature of Vex's divine power, suggesting it is 'borrowed' from The Many-Faced Lady, with unknown consequences should Vex betray her. The party also secured a Cloudy Glass Eye and a Ring of Little Deception as rewards.
