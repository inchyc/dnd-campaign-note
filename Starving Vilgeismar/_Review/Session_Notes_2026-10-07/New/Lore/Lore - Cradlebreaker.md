# 📜 Lore: Cradlebreaker

> [!quote] *"Mace is named cradle breaker. One hand life, the other death."*

## 🔍 Overview
A concept or artifact described in murals within [[Location - The Patriarch's Temple|The Patriarch's Temple]], embodying a duality of creation and destruction. It is depicted as a mace, symbolizing the power over life and death.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Depicted as a mace in engravings, embodying a duality of creation and destruction.
* **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten or actively suppressed due to its potentially dangerous theological implications.
* **Theological Significance:** Directly linked to the teachings of [[Lore - The Patriarch|The Patriarch]], representing a balance or cycle of existence.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The concept of a "Cradlebreaker" could be seen as heretical by the Church of the Many-Faced Lady, which emphasizes preservation and endurance.
