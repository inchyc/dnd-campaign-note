# 📜 Lore: The Patriarch

> [!quote] *"From blood comes life, from life comes death, from death, the next."*

## 🔍 Overview
An ancient, forgotten deity or philosophical figure whose temple was discovered by the party. The Patriarch's theology revolves around cycles of life, death, and rebirth, as evidenced by inscriptions, ritual bowls (Blood, Ash, Water), and murals depicting commandments like "The old must die to birth the new" and the "Cradlebreaker."

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** The temple dedicated to the Patriarch feels ancient and powerful, with mummified remains and preserved bodies suggesting a connection to life and death cycles.
* **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten or suppressed by the dominant faith of [[Lore/Church/Lore - The Many Faced Lady|The Many-Faced Lady]].
* **Theological Significance:** Represents a cyclical view of existence, where death is a necessary precursor to new life, contrasting with the Many-Faced Lady's focus on enduring the present.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The Patriarch's teachings, particularly those emphasizing the necessity of death for new birth, could be seen as deeply heretical and dangerous by the Church of the Many-Faced Lady, especially in a city struggling for survival.
