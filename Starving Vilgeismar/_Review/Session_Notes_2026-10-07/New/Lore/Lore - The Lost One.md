# 📜 Lore: The Lost One

> [!quote] *"The sibling who strayed, whose touch brings an unnatural stillness."*

## 🔍 Overview
A mysterious entity, referred to by [[NPC - Demire AKA The Traveller|Demire]] as "The Lost Sibling," confirmed to be responsible for turning [[Colm]] into an Undead. This entity is rumored to be one of the [[Lore - The Little Gods|Little Gods]] or [[Lore - The Old Gods|Old Gods]], but its exact nature and allegiance remain unknown.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Its influence is associated with the creation of Undead.
* **Public Perception:** Largely unknown to the general populace, its existence is likely a secret among powerful entities.
* **Theological Significance:** Its actions directly contradict the natural cycle of life and death, potentially challenging the tenets of [[Lore/Church/Lore - The Many Faced Lady|The Many-Faced Lady]].

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The Lost One's ability to create Undead poses a direct threat to the living and further destabilizes the city's already fragile state.
