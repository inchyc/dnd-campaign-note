# 📜 Lore: Dorn

> [!quote] *"The old must die to birth the new."*

## 🔍 Overview
A commandment or philosophical tenet depicted in murals within [[Location - The Patriarch's Temple|The Patriarch's Temple]], suggesting a cycle of renewal through destruction. This concept is central to the theology of [[Lore - The Patriarch|The Patriarch]].

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Depicted in murals within the temple, suggesting a strong, active presence in the Patriarch's theology.
* **Public Perception:** Unknown, likely forgotten.
* **Theological Significance:** A core principle of the Patriarch's faith, emphasizing transformation and the necessity of ending old forms for new ones to emerge.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> This philosophy directly contrasts with the Church of the Many-Faced Lady's emphasis on endurance and preservation, potentially inciting conflict or heresy.
