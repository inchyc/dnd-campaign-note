# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-06-03

## 📖 Executive Summary
The session began with the party discovering Rooprecht, who had been fed upon by a 'lesser vampire' but was confirmed not to have been turned. They then engaged with Lord Henrik Vaust, who revealed his intention to 'feast' on High Lady Seraphine Vaelor and sought the party's assistance, instructing them to trust Guard Captain Roderic Hale in the matter. The party then witnessed the tragic stabbing and death of Old Marta Kell in Beggar's Court, an event that saw the mysterious intervention of Demire (as a black cat) and a cryptic whisper from Roderic to the deceased Marta about 'our boy.'

Roderic, seemingly complicit with Vaust's plan, agreed to delay the city guards, allowing the party to rush to the Cathedral to warn the High Lady. Upon confronting High Lady Seraphine, she revealed a centuries-long 'game' between her lineage and Vaust, denying knowledge of Demire or the Old/Little Gods. Vaust, accompanied by his half-blood vampire right-hand man Lucas and another vampiric 'spawn' (a young girl), then infiltrated the High Lady's chambers, initiating combat. It was revealed that Vaust's animosity stems from a past High Lady, his first love, whose relationship was ended by the Many Faced Lady herself.
