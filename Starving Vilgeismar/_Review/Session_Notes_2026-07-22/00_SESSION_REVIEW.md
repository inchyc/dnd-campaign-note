# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-07-22

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party has spent a week aboard Demire's magical boat, The Eternal Wake, navigating waters where the fish are observed changing color from green to blue. Despite the boat's apparent sentience, the monkey captain, Glutenboss, struggles with traditional navigation. Following Nub Nub (The Collector), a creature gifted by Demire, the party arrived at a mysterious, seemingly 'copy-pasted' village in a jungle, entirely devoid of inhabitants. Inside the largest building, they discovered logbooks belonging to a person named The Keeper, detailing the village's sudden appearance, Nub Nub's origin, and the Keeper's crucial task of maintaining a nearby lighthouse to prevent a wider catastrophe. As they approached the lighthouse, they encountered a ravine bridge infested with zombies, leading to a combat encounter where Colm was pulled down.
