# 📜 Lore: Fish of the New World

> [!quote] "We need to keep going until fish are blue (currently green)."

## 🔍 Overview
Fish in the waters surrounding the party's current location are undergoing a color transformation, shifting from green to blue. This phenomenon suggests a significant environmental change or a magical influence in the new territories outside Vilgeismar.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Fish observed to be green, with an expectation for them to turn blue.
* **Public Perception:** The party is using this as a navigational or progress indicator.
* **Theological Significance:** Unknown, but could be related to the broader magical shifts post-Miracle.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The changing biology of local wildlife indicates an unstable or magically altered environment, the long-term effects of which are unknown.
