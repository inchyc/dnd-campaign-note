# 📜 Lore: Ashborn Clan

> [!quote] "Rich and fickle. Picky at best and a hinderance at worst."

## 🔍 Overview
A prominent clan, known for their wealth and unpredictable nature. They are considered difficult to deal with, often proving to be more of a hindrance than a help.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Public Perception:** Regarded as rich, fickle, and generally unhelpful.
* **Known Members:** [[Cedric]] is associated with this clan, bearing their crest.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> Their "picky" and "fickle" nature suggests they may be unreliable allies or even potential obstacles in times of crisis.
