# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-11-11

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party ventured into the Coral Market, encountering Prince Kaleen and his imposing mother, the Elven Queen. The Queen, a a figure of fear and vanity, immediately questioned Colm's "corpse" status and expressed disdain for her son. Before meeting the Queen, the party spoke with Guard Captain Roderic Hale, who, visibly injured, warned them about the Elven royalty and mentioned being saved by elves. A small, cat-sized entity, likely Demire, was observed in Roderic's room. Later, in the Queen's chambers, a "Shadow Weasel" was discovered, which the party attempted to tame. The Queen, seemingly unaware of the shadow creature, tasked the party with investigating the unmappable Under Caverns, where an "old ruler" slumbers, hinting at powerful, overwhelming magic. She provided a quartz-like nail as a token of authority and further discussed Colm's unique condition, linking it to the "First 13" and "The One Never Born."
