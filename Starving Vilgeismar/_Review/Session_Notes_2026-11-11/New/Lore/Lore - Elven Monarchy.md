# 📜 Lore: Elven Monarchy

> [!quote] *"King rules by Love, Queen by Fear, Prince is an Idiot."*

## 🔍 Overview
The ruling structure of the Elves, characterized by a King who rules by love, a Queen who rules by fear, and a seemingly ineffectual Prince.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Public Perception:** The [[Elven Queen]] is vain and feared, the [[Prince Kaleen]] is considered an "idiot."

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The contrasting ruling styles of the King and Queen, and the Prince's perceived incompetence, suggest internal tensions or vulnerabilities within the Elven leadership.
