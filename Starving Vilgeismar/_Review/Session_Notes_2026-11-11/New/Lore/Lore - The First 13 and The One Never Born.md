# 📜 Lore: The First 13 and The One Never Born

> [!quote] *"When the first 13 were born. The one that was never born was always looking for a corporal form."*

A cryptic piece of lore related to the origins of certain beings, possibly Changelings or other supernatural entities. It mentions "the first 13" being born, and "the one that was never born" constantly seeking a corporal form.
