# 📜 Lore: Burrow Kin

> [!quote] *"We are the shadows beneath the surface, the teeth in the dark."*

## 🔍 Overview
A term used to describe communities of Wererats, such as the one encountered in the war-torn town. They live in tunnels and have their own social structures.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
*   **Sensory Details:** (Not specified, but likely associated with rats and tunnels).
*   **Public Perception:** Likely feared and misunderstood by surface dwellers.
*   **Theological Significance:** [[NPCs/NPC - Mouse.md|Mouse]], a leader among them, is a follower of [[NPCs/NPC - Demire AKA The Traveller.md|Demire]].
