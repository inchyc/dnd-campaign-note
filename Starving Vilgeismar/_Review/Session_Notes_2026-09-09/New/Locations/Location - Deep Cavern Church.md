# 📍 Deep Cavern Church

> [!quote] **Atmosphere**
> An ancient, eerie church discovered deep within the [[Locations/Location - Tunnels (War-torn Town).md|Tunnels (War-torn Town)]]. It appears to be built a good while ago, with architectural similarities to a [[Lore/Church/Lore - The Many Faced Lady.md|Many-Faced Lady]] church, but dedicated to a different figure. The party's hymn intensified its creepy vibe.

### 🔍 Points of Interest
*   **The Landmark:** A reliquary depicting "[[Lore/Lore - Dorn.md|Dorn]] the Father" holding a baby and a mace (likely [[Lore/Lore - Cradlebreaker.md|Cradlebreaker]]).
*   **Supernatural Signs:** The atmosphere intensified when a hymn to the Many-Faced Lady was sung.
*   **Recent Activity:** People disappeared from the area about a week ago.

### 👥 Local Inhabitants
*   **Key NPC:** None currently, but the site is linked to [[Lore/Lore - Dorn.md|Dorn]] and potentially [[Lore/Lore - The Patriarch.md|The Patriarch]].
*   **Crowd Mood:** Desolate, ancient, and unsettling.
