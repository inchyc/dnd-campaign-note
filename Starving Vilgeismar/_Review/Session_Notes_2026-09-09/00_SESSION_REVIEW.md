# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-09-09

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party, having concluded their confrontation with Garron/Nub Nub, departed Vilgeismar aboard Demire's vessel, The Eternal Wake. They learned that 'Nub Nub' is a species of 'Hounds' or 'Collectors,' with one on each island, and Demire revealed he has lost 'someone.' Transferring to a new boat captained by Otto and crewed by the opportunistic Scarlet Stumpers, the party was dropped off at a war-torn town, bearing the scars of the same army that besieged Vilgeismar. Here, they discovered Rooprecht had transformed into a Wererat, now part of a 'Burrow Kin' community led by Mouse, a follower of Demire. Mouse informed them that Harcon, the 'Big One,' had consumed many Wererats. The party is now investigating the disappearance of people saved by the Wererats, venturing into deep caverns beneath the town. Within these tunnels, they found an ancient church, reminiscent of a Many-Faced Lady temple, featuring a reliquary dedicated to 'Dorn the Father,' depicted holding a baby and a mace. A hymn to the Many-Faced Lady sung by the party intensified the eerie atmosphere of this forgotten sanctuary.
