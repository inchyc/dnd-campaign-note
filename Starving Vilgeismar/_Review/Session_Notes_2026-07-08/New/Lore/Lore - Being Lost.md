# 📜 Lore: Being Lost

> [!quote] *"To be lost is not to be forgotten, but to be elsewhere."*

## 🔍 Overview
The entire population of Vilgeismar, save for a few, was magically teleported across the continent by [[NPC - Demire AKA The Traveller]] and [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]]. This event is referred to as 'Being Lost' by Demire, and the party is tasked with gathering the dispersed people.

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** The immediate aftermath involved a low resonance hum and a profound sense of displacement.
* **Public Perception:** Unknown, as the population is dispersed and likely disoriented across the continent.
* **Theological Significance:** A profound act by the [[Lore - The Many Faced Lady]], possibly a 'mercy' or a new phase of the Miracle, but with devastating immediate consequences. It redefines the city's existence and the fate of its people.

---

## ⚠️ Known Risks & Strain
> [!warning] **Point of Strain**
> The party is tasked with gathering the dispersed population and retaking the city, a monumental and dangerous task. The city itself is now empty and occupied by the besieging army.
