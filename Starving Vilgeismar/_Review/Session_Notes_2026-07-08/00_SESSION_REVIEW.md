# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-07-08

## 📖 Executive Summary
The session began in intense combat within the High Lady's Chambers, where the party killed Lord Henrik Vaust. During the confrontation, Demire (in cat form) appeared, warning the party. A shocking revelation occurred as High Lady Seraphine Vaelor transformed into the Many Faced Lady, consuming Vaust's vampiric spawn. Simultaneously, the besieging army breached the city, indicating the Veil's failure.

Demire and the Many Faced Lady then enacted a powerful ritual, causing the entire population of Vilgeismar to disappear, teleported to various locations across the continent. The party escaped through a secret passage to Demire's hidden boat, The Eternal Wake, captained by a magical monkey named Glutenboss. Aewren, however, ran back into the now-empty city, presumably after Valcara.

The party, now 'Lost' along with the city's populace, was tasked by Demire with gathering the dispersed citizens and eventually retaking Vilgeismar, which now stands empty save for the invading army.
