# 📜 Lore: Hedge Magic

> [!quote] *"A crude, often instinctual form of magic, sometimes bearing unexpected flavors."

## 🔍 Overview
A form of magic distinct from traditional arcane or divine practices. It was recently detected by [[Vex]]'s [[The Silent Witness|Divine Sense]] in a nearby building, described as "evil Hedge Magic with Divine flavours."

---

## 🧬 Phenomenon & Observations
* **Sensory Details:** Detected as "evil" with "Divine flavours" by [[Vex]]'s [[The Silent Witness|Divine Sense]].
* **Public Perception:** (Unknown, likely varied or misunderstood)
* **Theological Significance:** The presence of "Divine flavours" within an "evil" form of magic is a significant anomaly, potentially hinting at corrupted divine influence or a perversion of sacred energies.
