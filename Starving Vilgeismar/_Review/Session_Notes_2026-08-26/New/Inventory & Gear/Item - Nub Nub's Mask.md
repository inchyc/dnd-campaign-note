# 🍯 Item: Nub Nub's Mask

## 📝 Description
* **Appearance**: A mask, seemingly made of hardened hide and bone. When worn, it causes the wearer to transform into an owlbear-like creature.
* **Sensory**: Feels rough and slightly warm to the touch.
