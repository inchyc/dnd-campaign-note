# ⚔️ Session Review: Session Notes 2026-08-26

## 📖 Executive Summary
The party confronted the 'Evil Hedge Magic/Divine Creature' at the new town, discovering it was a memory-lost [[Garron Thrice-Garrotted]]. He joined them on their journey to a massive lighthouse, guided by [[staged:NPC - Nub Nub|Nub Nub]]. Inside the lighthouse, they solved a puzzle involving eight statues and a green flame, lighting the top of the structure. During this, Garron betrayed the party, killing Nub Nub and donning [[Item - Nub Nub's Mask|Nub Nub's Mask]], which transformed him into an owlbear-like creature. The party was forced to kill Garron. They recovered several items from his body, including the mask, a logbook, cleavers, a peculiar eye, a magical necklace, and a jewelled belt, along with 34 gold pieces. Following the confrontation, [[NPC - Demire AKA The Traveller|Demire]] was observed back on [[staged:The Eternal Wake|The Eternal Wake]], which began to move.
