## 👁️ Atmosphere & Senses
* **Sights:** Muddy, rutted roads. Simple log buildings built on top of old fieldstone ruins. A strong smell of manure and woodsmoke.
* **Sounds:** The clanging of the smithy, shouting miners, the distant roar of the Triboar Trail.
* **Vibe:** A frontier town trying to civilize itself. Tension hangs in the air regarding the dragon.
